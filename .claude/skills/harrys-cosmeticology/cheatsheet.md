# Cheatsheet

Decision rules and key numbers, grouped by book section. Numbers are as stated in the book (2015); check current regulations before relying on legal limits.

## Marketing & Fragrance

- If the label must not say "fragrance" → request an INCI blend up front (natural INCI if "all natural"). (v1-p1-2-fragrance-selection)
- If the product goes into the mouth/lips or is a children's toy → GRAS fragrance. (v1-p1-2-fragrance-selection)
- If sold in the EU and no allergens may appear on the label → request "EU Allergen Label Free" (no natural essential oils). (v1-p1-2-fragrance-selection)
- If a bath oil splits with fragrance → switch carrier from DPG to isopropyl myristate. (v1-p1-2-fragrance-selection)
- If a vanilla/gourmand fragrance yellows the product → add a chelating agent and keep slightly acidic. (v1-p1-2-fragrance-selection)
- If a product contains ammonia (hair color) → expect only minor masking from fragrance (trigeminal + high volatility). (v1-p1-2-fragrance-selection)
- If a tested scent underperforms at launch → fix name/bottle/positioning before reformulating. (v1-p1-3-fragrance-packaging-design)
- If calling an ingredient "innovative" → check whether it changes consumer behavior; most are continuous innovations. (v1-p1-1-marketing-concepts)
- 80-90% — share of new products that fail (v1-p1-1-marketing-concepts)
- 10% / 50% — typical distributor / retailer share of retail price (v1-p1-1-marketing-concepts)
- 20 years — U.S. patent exclusivity (v1-p1-1-marketing-concepts)
- 1-20% — essential oil content of a standard fragrance (v1-p1-2-fragrance-selection)
- 0.001-0.100% — concretes/absolutes in a fragrance (v1-p1-2-fragrance-selection)
- 5-20% — carrier in a traditional fragrance (v1-p1-2-fragrance-selection)
- 70-80% — surfactant in a WS fragrance; WD 30-50% (v1-p1-2-fragrance-selection)
- 3-10x — cost of natural vs traditional fragrance (v1-p1-2-fragrance-selection)
- 11 — IFRA product categories; 26 — EU dermal allergens (v1-p1-2-fragrance-selection)
- 6.8 — histidine pKa (cytoplasm pH); pH shifts modulate protein activity (v1-p1-4-molecular-biology-gene-expression)
- >200 — genes transcribed under Nrf2 control (v1-p1-4-molecular-biology-gene-expression)

## Regulation & IP

- If a claim mentions hair regrowth, cellulite, varicose veins, whitening/darkening or cell regeneration → product becomes a drug in the US (and is not a cosmetic in the EU/KSA). (v1-p2-2-us-eu-regulation)
- If product stability ≤30 months → EU label needs a minimum durability date; if >30 months → PAO symbol. (v1-p2-2-us-eu-regulation)
- If an ingredient enters the EU at >1 t/yr and is not a polymer or natural substance → needs REACH registration (ask supplier for number, else register or replace). (v1-p2-2-us-eu-regulation)
- If the product contains a nanomaterial → EU notification 6 months before launch and "(nano)" on the label. (v1-p2-3-4-nanomaterials-regulation)
- If exporting to the CU and the product is in a high-risk category (children's, whitening, hair dyes, nano, peels, etc.) → State Registration; else Declaration of Conformity tied to the importer. (v1-p2-3-1-russia-customs-union)
- If an ingredient is not in China's IECIC → treat it as a new ingredient (years-long approval); reformulate. (v1-p2-3-3-china)
- If shipping to KSA → one CoC per shipment; exact country of origin on product and pack. (v1-p2-3-2-saudi-arabia)
- If you plan to publish or partner → file the patent application first. (v1-p2-4-intellectual-property)
- 100 — minimum Margin of Safety (NOAEL/SED) (v1-p2-2-us-eu-regulation)
- 0.001% leave-on / 0.01% rinse-off — EU fragrance allergen labeling thresholds (v1-p2-2-us-eu-regulation)
- 1000 cfu/g (500 cfu/g eye area) — common industry micro limits (v1-p2-2-us-eu-regulation)
- 0.1% — US max hexachlorophene; 65 ppm Hg eye-area preservative, <1 ppm Hg elsewhere (v1-p2-2-us-eu-regulation)
- 1/16 in (1/32 in if <12 sq in) — US ingredient-list letter height (v1-p2-2-us-eu-regulation)
- pH 5.0–9.0 creams / 5.5–8.5 liquid mascara-eyeliner; As 5, Hg 1, Pb 5 mg/kg — CU limits (v1-p2-3-1-russia-customs-union)
- >0.15% F (>0.05% liquids); 0.1–6.0% H2O2 — CU State Registration triggers for oral care (v1-p2-3-1-russia-customs-union)
- AHA ≤10% at pH ≥3.5, cautions at ≥3% leave-on; salicylic acid ≤2% (≤3% rinse-off hair, <0.5% as preservative); retinol ≤1%; urea ≤10%; ZnO <25% — KSA limits (v1-p2-3-2-saudi-arabia)
- <8% fragrance oil in ethanol perfume → ≤250 ml, non-removable pump, Arabic "for external use only" (KSA) (v1-p2-3-2-saudi-arabia)
- 1–100 nm — nanoscale range in EU/US/Canada definitions (v1-p2-3-4-nanomaterials-regulation)
- 20 years utility patent / 14 years US design patent / ~10 years utility model / 18 months publication (v1-p2-4-intellectual-property)

## Skin

- If an active is >500 Da → don't expect passive SC penetration; use carriers/follicular route or claim SC-level protection (v1-p3-1-skin-structure)
- If formulating for EP V–X skin → avoid irritants (fragrance, alcohol, harsh exfoliation), caution with retinoids/HQ/deep peels to prevent PIH and keloids (v1-p3-2-1-global-skin-classification)
- If a lightening claim → pair with mandatory daily broad-spectrum SPF 30 (v1-p3-2-3-asian-ethnic-skin)
- If targeting elderly skin → focus on barrier recovery, dryness/NMF, elasticity — not "weakened static barrier" (v1-p3-2-4-aging-elderly-skin)
- If marketing a "cosmeceutical" → keep claims cosmetic; term has no FD&C legal status (v1-p3-2-4-aging-elderly-skin)
- 4% — FDA-approved hydroquinone cream for hyperpigmentation (v1-p3-2-2-skin-of-color-disorders)
- 0.05% — tretinoin significantly improves photo-damage at 24 weeks (v1-p3-2-4-aging-elderly-skin)
- 0.15% retinol / 0.3% retinyl propionate — wrinkles and pigment improvement at 12 weeks (v1-p3-2-4-aging-elderly-skin)
- 8% lactic or glycolic acid — beats vehicle on photo-aging; ~2% salicylic acid for BHA (v1-p3-2-4-aging-elderly-skin)
- 3% ascorbate — reduced wrinkles only in an optimized formulation (v1-p3-2-4-aging-elderly-skin)
- 30–70% glycolic peel — melasma, ~6 sessions 10 days apart (v1-p3-2-3-asian-ethnic-skin)
- 42 days / 14 days — epidermis / SC renewal time; SC turnover 19.8 → 28.1 days young → >60 y (v1-p3-1-skin-structure; v1-p3-2-4-aging-elderly-skin)
- 80–85% → ~35% — water content basal layer → granulosum/SC border (v1-p3-1-skin-structure)
- 14 days — sebocyte cycle; allow before judging sebum-modifying products (v1-p3-1-skin-structure)

## Hair

- If straightening with an iron yields little effect at 100-150 °C → raise to 170-210 °C; the set needs Tm/Tg transitions, not dehydration (v1-p3-3-4-hair-shape-changes)
- If a curl is very tight (African hair) → thio or bisulfite will revert; alkaline OH- relaxer or more memory deletion is needed (v1-p3-3-4-hair-shape-changes)
- If a volumizer loses its wave after blow-drying → speed drying / reduce evaporation-retarding ingredients (v1-p3-3-4-hair-shape-changes)
- If styling resin flakes → Tg too high / under-plasticized; if hold flows in humidity → Tg too low or moisture-sensitive (v1-p3-3-4-hair-shape-changes)
- If consumer complains of "dry hair" → address surface roughness/cuticle, not just cortex moisture (v1-p3-3-1-hair-physical-chemical-properties)
- If cuticles are buckled → liquid water contact (rinse/condensation) aids recovery; ambient humidity does not (v1-p3-3-1-hair-physical-chemical-properties)
- If gray >50% → permanent color needed; demi-permanent may not cover (v1-p3-3-3-hair-aging)
- 7-9% thioglycolate, pH ~9.5; 3% H2O2 neutralizer — permanent wave (v1-p3-3-4-hair-shape-changes)
- pH 11.5-14.0, 1.5-3% hydroxide, 15-20 min — alkaline relaxer (v1-p3-3-4-hair-shape-changes)
- Tm ~156 °C, Tg ~144 °C — hair thermal transitions (v1-p3-3-1-hair-physical-chemical-properties)
- >80 °C begins disulfide breakage; >5% strain starts alpha->beta — water-set limits (v1-p3-3-4-hair-shape-changes)
- ~30% water uptake at 100% RH — hair sorption capacity (v1-p3-3-1-hair-physical-chemical-properties)
- 100-150 hairs/day shed; 0.3-0.4 mm/day scalp growth; 80,000-150,000 scalp follicles (v1-p3-3-2-hair-follicle-biology)
- Minoxidil 2% / 5% twice daily; 5% men only, 45% more effective at 48 weeks (v1-p3-3-3-hair-aging)
- Lash: anagen 1-4 months, 60-85% telogen, 0.12 mm/day; conditioners raise anagen fraction 20 -> 90% (v1-p3-3-5-eyelashes)

## Nails, Nose, Mouth, Lips, Feminine Care & Sustainability

- If a consumer reports a new or changing dark band in a nail → refer to a dermatologist (melanoma risk), do not treat cosmetically (v1-p3-4-nails)
- If a lifted nail turns green or yellow → Pseudomonas or Candida colonization under onycholysis, not a stain (v1-p3-4-nails)
- If an oral product claims anticaries, antigingivitis or desensitizing → it is an OTC drug (US anticaries: 21 CFR 310/355/369); periodontitis claims are not OTC (v1-p3-6-1-mouth-oral-care)
- If formulating a peroxide whitener for Canada → not permitted (v1-p3-6-1-mouth-oral-care)
- If designing a vulvar product → no propylene glycol, parabens, fragrance or petrolatum bases (v1-p3-8-feminine-rejuvenation)
- If choosing an extraction solvent → water/ethanol keep spent plant biodegradable; glycerine is only a carrier (sus-p12-1-sustainability)
- If sourcing wild botanicals → check CITES, Nagoya/CBD benefit sharing, and a sustainable-harvest plan (sus-p12-1-sustainability)
- 0.5–1.2 mm/week — fingernail growth; toenail 0.2–0.4 mm/week; full replacement 5–6 months (finger) vs 12–18 months (toe) (v1-p3-4-nails)
- pH 5.5 — critical pH for enamel demineralization; resting saliva 6.5–7.2, stimulated up to ~8.0 (v1-p3-6-1-mouth-oral-care)
- ≥0.4 — Z′ factor threshold for HTS-suitable OR assays (v1-p3-5-nose-olfaction)
- 57/25/15/2/1% — sebum triglycerides/wax esters/squalene/cholesterol esters/cholesterol (v1-p3-7-lip-skin)
- ≤12% water — dried plant stability limit; 75°C drying stops myrosinase in erysimum (sus-p12-1-sustainability)
- ≤40°C, 4 Å zeolites — zeodration drying conditions (sus-p12-1-sustainability)

## Ingredients I

- If emulsion contains AHA/BHA → do not use soap-stabilized (pH > 7) systems; use phosphate esters or nonionics (v2-p4-1-1-surfactants)
- If formulating amphoterics → move pH away from the zwitterionic point (v2-p4-1-1-surfactants)
- If lipstick sweats at room temperature → suspect incompatibility, cut nonpolar oils, add bentonite/hectorite master gel (v2-p4-1-2-lipstick-ingredients)
- If HA must reach dermis → use small fragments (≤50 kDa) or hydrophobized HA; >500 kDa stays on surface (v2-p4-1-3-hyaluronic-acid)
- If product aw ≥ 0.70 → chemical preservative required (v2-p4-1-7-1-antimicrobial-preservatives)
- If using vitamin E for skin benefit → keep α-tocopherol low and add co-antioxidants to avoid pro-oxidation (v2-p4-1-7-2-antioxidants-shelf-life)
- If product is an O/W emulsion, soap or shampoo → add a chelator; test OSI on oils + PV on finished product (v2-p4-1-7-2-antioxidants-shelf-life)
- If wanting turmeric benefits without staining → use tetrahydrocurcumin (v2-p4-1-5-multifunctional-botanicals)
- If selecting probiotic ingredients → use non-live PDBs verified by defensin ELISA; caution for psoriasis-prone users (v2-p4-1-4-2-probiotics)
- 1–5% — emulsifier level in creams/lotions (v2-p4-1-1-surfactants)
- 6–12% anionic / ~5% amphoteric / ~2% amide — typical shampoo surfactant split (v2-p4-1-1-surfactants)
- <10% (often ~5%) solids — hair conditioner (v2-p4-1-1-surfactants)
- 65–75°C — lipstick melt-point window (v2-p4-1-2-lipstick-ingredients)
- 0.01–0.2% — typical HA level in cosmetics (v2-p4-1-3-hyaluronic-acid)
- 0.91 / 0.80 / 0.70 aw — lower growth limits for most bacteria / most molds / some organisms (v2-p4-1-7-1-antimicrobial-preservatives)
- 40–50% glycerin or sorbitol — inhibits nearly all cosmetic-relevant microbes (v2-p4-1-7-1-antimicrobial-preservatives)
- 30–40°C bacteria, 20–25°C fungi — optimal growth temperatures (v2-p4-1-7-1-antimicrobial-preservatives)
- 0.25% THC vs 4% hydroquinone — THC more effective, no adverse reactions vs 50% (v2-p4-1-5-multifunctional-botanicals)
- 31°C / 74 bar — CO2 critical point for supercritical extraction (v2-p4-1-5-multifunctional-botanicals)
- PF > 1 — OSI protection factor indicating effective antioxidant (v2-p4-1-7-2-antioxidants-shelf-life)

## Ingredients II (Rheology, Silicones, Whiteners, Marine, Cellulite)

- If you need a clear gel → carbomer/synthetic acrylic polymer (0.1–0.5%), polyethylene for anhydrous; if opaque → clays/organoclays (v2-p4-2-1-rheological-additives)
- If silica or large polymers are in the batch → avoid high shear after addition (v2-p4-2-1-rheological-additives)
- If thickening oils/silicones/esters → organoclay pre-dispersion first choice (v2-p4-2-1-rheological-additives)
- If gelling 60–90% alcohol sanitizer with carbomer → 1 wt% carbomer + triisopropanolamine to pH 7.5; >90% use PEG-15 cocamine (v2-p4-2-2-rheology-modifiers-consumer-perception)
- If using copolyol in SLES base → choose low-MW dimethicone copolyol (v2-p4-2-3-1-silicones)
- If lipstick sweats → melt unpigmented base; melt range ≥4 °C and haze = incompatibility; add alkyl/multi-domain silicone to tighten to 1–2 °C (v2-p4-2-3-1-silicones)
- If anti-cellulite claim → block estrogen, inhibit MMPs, mobilize fat, rebuild collagen; study ≥90–180 days (v2-p4-2-6-cellulite)
- pH 3.5–5.0 / 5.5–10 / >10 — carbomer viscosity rising / plateau / falling (v2-p4-2-2-rheology-modifiers-consumer-perception)
- ±1 °C — enough to significantly change aqueous viscosity readings (v2-p4-2-2-rheology-modifiers-consumer-perception)
- ≥80 °C — heating needed for polyethylene gel structure (v2-p4-2-1-rheological-additives)
- PEG MW <700 — liquid at room temperature (v2-p4-2-1-rheological-additives)
- ≤200 cSt / ≥350 cSt — dimethicone soluble / insoluble in anhydrous ethanol (v2-p4-2-3-1-silicones)
- <3% — typical silicone fluid use level (v2-p4-2-3-1-silicones)
- 5–50 µm — cosmetic silicone elastomer particle size, nano-free (v2-p4-2-3-2-silicone-elastomers)
- QD levels: kojic 1%, arbutin 3%, MAP 3%, ascorbyl glucoside 2%, ellagic 0.5%, rucinol 0.3%, linoleic 0.1%, AMP 3% (v2-p4-2-4-skin-whiteners)
- 1% liposomal photolyase — 45% fewer UV dimers after 1 h (v2-p4-2-5-marine-ingredients)
- Genistein 0.5–1%, chrysin 1%, DIM ≈0.5%, grapeseed/pine bark 1.4% — anti-cellulite starting levels (v2-p4-2-6-cellulite)

## Ingredients III — Anti-Aging Actives

- If users discontinue a retinoid due to irritation → switch to retinol (start at 0.1%), use nightly with moisturizer, avoid salicylic-acid/alcohol products, and add sunscreen (v2-p4-3-1-topical-retinoids)
- If the target consumer has sensitive, rosacea-, eczema- or atopic-prone skin → choose gluconolactone, lactobionic/maltobionic acid or NAG instead of glycolic acid (v2-p4-3-5-hydroxy-acids)
- If an active must work alongside retinol or other low-pH-sensitive ingredients → use neutral-pH NAG or N-acetylamino acids rather than AHAs (v2-p4-3-5-hydroxy-acids)
- If vitamin C must be in an acidic formula → use ascorbyl glucoside; SAP/MAP need pH >7 plus a chelator (v2-p4-3-7-antioxidants)
- If a cleanser must preserve NMF → use acyl glutamate/glycinate surfactants (10% sodium lauroyl glutamate removes 52% AA vs 62% for SLES, 70% for soap) (v2-p4-3-4-amino-acids)
- If a peptide is water-soluble but must penetrate the SC → add a palmitoyl group (v2-p4-3-2-peptides)
- If choosing a humectant for dry/low-humidity conditions → L-proline retains the most water at 35% RH; Na-PCA/Na lactate absorb most at 65% RH (v2-p4-3-4-amino-acids)
- pH = pKa (3.8 for glycolic) — 50% free acid; 10% glycolic at pH 3.8 = 5% bioavailable (v2-p4-3-5-hydroxy-acids)
- 4–10% — cosmetic AHA range proven against photo-damage; typical product pH 3.5–4.5 (v2-p4-3-5-hydroxy-acids)
- 8% pH 3.8 — standard glycolic/lactobionic/maltobionic test formulas; 20% pH 3.8 maltobionic nonirritating in RIPT (v2-p4-3-5-hydroxy-acids)
- 0.5–1% — NaCl/MgSO₄ normally added to stabilize W/O and W/Si emulsions (watch AHA salts) (v2-p4-3-5-hydroxy-acids)
- 1–2% retinol (benefit from 0.1%) — typical OTC level (v2-p4-3-1-topical-retinoids)
- 50 ppm palmitoyl pentapeptide-3 — significant periorbital wrinkle benefit vs vehicle (v2-p4-3-2-peptides)
- 3 ppm / 30 ppm Zinc PCA — 69.3% / 77% AP-1 inhibition in fibroblasts (v2-p4-3-4-amino-acids)
- 1:1:1 / 3:2:1 glycine:proline:alanine — 2× / 4× collagen production (v2-p4-3-4-amino-acids)
- 15% L-ascorbic + 1% α-tocopherol + 0.5% ferulic acid — proven in-vivo photoprotective combination (v2-p4-3-7-antioxidants)
- 4–5× — UVA/UVB rise in MMP-1/MMP-3 in fibroblasts while TIMP-1 barely rises (v2-p4-3-7-antioxidants)
- 2% — Uttwiler Spatlauber apple stem-cell extract level in vitro and in products (v2-p4-3-6-growth-factors-stem-cells)

## Fundamentals of Skin Anti-Aging

- If claiming anti-glycation reversal → pre-glycate with MGO before applying the active (v2-p5-4-glycation-proteasome-telomeres)
- If formulating anti-glycation → avoid monosaccharides (e.g., honey); lower pH reduces glycation (v2-p5-4-glycation-proteasome-telomeres)
- If self-assessment claims ("87% saw fewer lines") → control for moisturizer effect with vehicle (v2-p5-3-anti-senescence)
- If cold-processing peptide derivatives → check solubility first, presolubilize in a liquid component (v2-p5-3-anti-senescence)
- If product targets hyperproliferation (psoriasis) → consider sirtuin inhibition (nicotinamide), not activation (v2-p5-5-sirtuins-and-skin)
- If positioning a repair/regeneration active → night application (higher penetration, peak DNA repair, 30× proliferation) (v2-p5-7-skin-chronobiology)
- If stimulating proteasome → weigh caveat (proteasome inhibition is anti-inflammatory/anti-cancer elsewhere; p53 degradation) (v2-p5-4-glycation-proteasome-telomeres)
- If "hydration" claim → judge ICW vs ECW, not total water; edema is wasted water (v2-p5-2-cellular-water-principle)
- pH 4.5–6.5 — preferred range for glutaminylglutamine amide anti-senescence peptides (v2-p5-3-anti-senescence)
- 300 mOsm — osmolarity threshold above which cells secrete IL-8, IL-6, IL-1β, TNF-α (v2-p5-3-anti-senescence)
- 10 mJ/cm² UVB — non-cytotoxic dose that suppresses keratinocyte clock genes ~20 h and disrupts SIRT3/4 and ATP (v2-p5-7-skin-chronobiology)
- ~25% — higher TEWL at night vs morning/afternoon (v2-p5-7-skin-chronobiology)
- 0.96 — correlation of DNA methylation signatures with chronological age (v2-p5-6-epigenetics-of-skin-aging)
- 15 years — collagen half-life; 3× more CML/CEL/pentosidine at 80 vs 20 (v2-p5-4-glycation-proteasome-telomeres)
- 10× — cortisol-induced collagen loss in skin vs other tissues (v2-p5-8-stress-sleep-epigenetic-orthodontics)
- AHI >30 — severe OSA; 5–15 mild, 15–30 moderate (v2-p5-8-stress-sleep-epigenetic-orthodontics)
- 2% day / 4% night — Albizia julibrissin extract levels in vehicle-controlled trials (v2-p5-4-glycation-proteasome-telomeres)
