# Part 3.3.5: Eyelashes: Anatomy and Conditioners for Increasing Length and Fullness/Thickness
**Authors**: Susan Lin, M.D.

## Core Idea
Eyelashes differ from scalp hair: short, curved, few, with a very short anagen and most follicles resting in telogen. Lash conditioners work by shifting follicles into and lengthening anagen. Topical prostaglandin analogues (first noticed as a side effect of glaucoma drops) produce the largest gains; later generations use prostaglandin-free peptides/cytokines and, most recently, adipose-derived stem cell secreted proteins, with a growing interest in extending these to brows and scalp.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Three generations of lash conditioners**:
  - 1st wave (early 2000s): oils + vitamins, modest results. Then 2007 prostaglandin-analogue conditioners (e.g., dechloro ethylcloprostenolamide).
  - 2nd generation: prostaglandin-free — cytokines/bioactive peptides, antioxidants, prostaglandin precursors, vitamins; similar effects but longer use needed.
  - 3rd generation: peptides/proteins secreted by adipose-derived stem cells; no published efficacy data yet.
  - When to use: positioning a lash/brow growth product and its expected onset and claim strength.
- **Safety package for a periocular active** (as done for dechloro ethylcloprostenolamide):
  - How: human RIPT (n = 209; no irritation/sensitization), in vitro mammalian cytotoxicity (non-cytotoxic), LLNA (negative for delayed sensitization), Ames test in five Salmonella strains (nonmutagenic), 4-week ophthalmologist-supervised ocular safety in contact lens wearers, non-wearers and self-assessed sensitive eyes (serial exams, questionnaires; no change in intraocular pressure, lacrimal duct, conjunctiva).
- **Consumer self-assessment statistics**: Z1 = favorable (completely + somewhat agree), Z2 = unfavorable; compare frequencies by Chi-squared test at 5%.

## Key Concepts
- **Lash dimensions**: 4-14 mm long (varies by location, race, age); follicles ~2 mm deep in dermis; eyelid has thin epidermis and no hypodermis.
- **Lash count**: upper lid ~300-400 in multiple rows (triangular/pyramid arrangement); lower lid ~100-150, shorter.
- **Curvature**: from asymmetric keratin 38 distribution in the bulb; most curved in African, then Caucasian, least in Asian lashes.
- **Glands**: no arrector pili muscle. ~50 glands upper lid, ~25 lower. Zeis = unilobular sebaceous, oil into mid-follicle; Moll = modified apocrine sweat glands; Meibomian = sebaceous, lipid for tear film. Melanocytes dense in eyelid basal epidermis, ORS and lash follicle.
- **Lash cycle**: anagen 1-4 months, telogen 4-8 months (scalp anagen 2-6 years, telogen ~3 months). Growth 0.12 mm/day (± 0.05). 60-85% of lash follicles in telogen vs ~15% on scalp.
- **Conditioner effect**: lashes in anagen increased from 20 to 90%, anagen extended from 4 months to 2 years; mechanism possibly intracellular cAMP regulation (unproven).
- **Prostaglandin analogue hypertrichosis**: glaucoma drops (latanoprost, bimatoprost) increase lash length, number, thickness and extra rows. Latanoprost: +0.67 mm mean after 6 months (20 adults); +0.7-0.8 mm after 2-10 weeks (17 patients). Rare side effects of ophthalmic forms: iris pigmentation, periocular skin darkening; bimatoprost hyperemia peaks day 1 then returns near baseline.
- **Choy et al. RCT (dechloro ethylcloprostenolamide applied to lash roots)**: 34 volunteers, double-blind, 4 weeks daily. Length gains: groups 1.69, 1.94, 1.44 mm vs placebo 0.63 mm (all significant). 24% reported thicker/fuller lashes; 15 mild transient irritation, 1 moderate (discontinued); no discoloration. Gains exceeded literature values for ophthalmic drops.
- **Longer-term data**: 21 patients at 4 months approached ~80% length increase from baseline; one alopecia areata patient gained 100% length in 4 weeks (bimatoprost earlier gave minimal effect). Among 70,000 users in year one: no confirmed serious adverse reaction or iris discoloration.
- **User observations**: new hairs at medial/lateral eye corners; light-lashed users see length/thickness first, then color; 3 Hispanic users had lashes fall out in first 2 months with regrowth over 2 months (likely catagen follicles entering anagen).
- **Brow/scalp extension study**: 30 subjects (50/50 sex, 18-45), men Hamilton II-III, women Ludwig I-2 to I-4; follow-up days 42, 84, 121. Results: 88% little/minimal discomfort, 96% easy to apply, 71% helped growth (17% noted at 4 weeks, 38% at 8, 8% at 12), 50% thickness, 63% volume, 46% softness, 75% would recommend. Positioned as adjuvant to hair-loss therapy.
- **Future**: adipose-derived stem cell secreted proteins regenerated scalp hair topically (Fukuoka et al.); preliminary injection of stem-cell-derived purified growth factors gave results after one treatment in 4 weeks; possible for lashes/brows.

## Formulation & Practical Guidance
- Apply lash actives directly to the lash root line rather than as eye drops; this gave larger absolute and relative gains than ophthalmic use.
- Expect onset in weeks (significant at 4 weeks for prostaglandin analogue); prostaglandin-free versions need longer use.
- For periocular claims, run RIPT, cytotoxicity, LLNA, Ames, and an ophthalmologist-supervised ocular study including contact lens wearers and sensitive eyes, measuring intraocular pressure.
- Warn users of possible transient mild irritation and temporary initial lash shedding; monitor for iris/periocular pigmentation (known with ophthalmic analogues).
- For consumer perception studies, use Z1/Z2 favorable/unfavorable groupings with Chi-squared at 5%.

## Mental Models
- Lash length is limited by anagen duration, not growth rate: the lever is moving telogen follicles into a longer anagen.
- Drug side effects can seed cosmetic actives (glaucoma prostaglandins -> lash growth), but bring the drug's safety questions with them.
- A small resting-heavy follicle population (60-85% telogen) leaves large headroom for anagen-promoting actives.

## Anti-patterns
- **Relying on oils and vitamins alone**: only modest lash improvement.
- **Using ophthalmic drops as the delivery model**: lower lash gains and pigmentation side effects compared with root-line application of a dedicated analogue.
