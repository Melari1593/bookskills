# Part 4.3.6: Cytokines, Growth Factors, and Stem Cells — Newest Approaches to Younger-Looking Skin
**Authors**: Sarah A. Malerich, Nils Krueger, Neil S. Sadick (Sadick Dermatology Research Group)

## Core Idea
Aging skin resembles a chronic wound: fewer fibroblasts, less growth factor and ECM, more MMP-driven collagen breakdown. Topical growth factors and cytokines (often as secreted mixtures from cultured fibroblasts) and stem-cell extracts (adipose-derived or plant) aim to restore the skin's own repair and matrix-building signalling. Clinical data show wrinkle, texture and epidermal-thickness gains, but penetration of these large molecules and rigorous human data remain the open questions.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Intrinsic vs extrinsic aging**: intrinsic — inevitable; thinner skin, fewer fibroblasts, thinner vessels, declining collagen and elastin synthesis. Extrinsic — mainly UV, plus smoking, pollution, poor nutrition; overstimulates epidermal/dermal cells and degrades collagen, elastin and ECM.
  - When to use: framing which mechanism a cosmeceutical claims to counter.
- **Chronic-wound hypothesis of aging**: growth factors that drive wound healing (fibroblast and keratinocyte proliferation → ECM formation) are supplied topically to restart repair.
  - How: combine multiple growth factors and cytokines, since they regulate each other through healing.
- **Requirements for an effective cosmeceutical**: penetrate into and through the stratum corneum while staying active; give visible benefit without harming barrier function.
- **Stem-cell sourcing**: human adipose tissue (mesenchymal stem cells), sheep and cow placentas, plants (Uttwiler Spatlauber apple, jasmine, lilac seeds); extracted then cultured to yield extracts rich in growth factors and cytokines.

## Key Concepts
- **Aged skin**: ↓ ECM proteins, ↑ collagen degradation, ↓ fibroblasts; ↓ immune response, wound repair and fiber synthesis. Free radicals activate MMPs and inhibit their tissue inhibitors.
- **Growth factors**: secreted by fibroblasts, keratinocytes, melanocytes; immune-regulating ones are cytokines, also involved in repair.
- **Penetration**: growth factors/cytokines are large hydrophilic molecules that don't easily cross the barrier; proposed entry via hair follicles, sweat glands and compromised skin; once in the epidermis they can signal to dermal cells.
- **Cancer concern**: theoretical carcinogenic potential linked mainly to VEGF (receptors on some melanoma cells); studies conflict. An FDA investigation found that much higher concentrations than in topical products would be required; minimal penetration → topical use unlikely to affect cancer growth.
- **TGF-β1**: topical use restored damaged skin, ↑ epidermal thickness and new collagen, reduced sun damage (more in severe photo-damage), reduced wrinkles; patients reported better elasticity, texture, hydration.
- **Neonatal dermal fibroblast secretions**: >110 growth factors, cytokines and matrix proteins; ↓ wrinkles/fine lines, ↑ epidermal thickness (Fitzpatrick II and higher); effect lasted ≥3 months after stopping; improved roughness and shadows, greatest in severe photo-damage.
- **Processed skin-cell proteins (PSP)**: from cultured fetal dermal fibroblasts; >100 growth factors and cytokines; fetal fibroblasts linked to scarless repair and used in pediatric wound closure. Periorbital/perioral wrinkles reduced after 2 months twice daily; dark circles reduced (thicker skin hides vessels); significant from 30 days.
- **Adipose-derived stem cells (ASCs) and conditioned medium (ASC-CM)**: ↑ collagen synthesis, ↓ MMP-1, ↑ fibroblast migration, protect fibroblasts from UVB oxidative stress, enhance antioxidant mechanisms, anti-inflammatory (cytokines modulate lymphocytes), inhibit cell-death enzymes, dose-dependent lightening via ↓ tyrosinase and TRP1 (of high interest in Eastern societies valuing whitening); can replace soft tissue by induction into adipocytes.
- **Uttwiler Spatlauber apple stem cells**: Swiss apple with long storage/oxidation resistance; 2% extract delayed senescence in H₂O₂-stressed fibroblasts; prolonged hair-follicle growth phase by 4 days; 2% products reduced wrinkles in as little as 2 weeks. Mechanism and duration unproven.
- **"Plant stem cell" naming caveat**: some products are extracts of the plant's stem, with no undifferentiated cells (often just antioxidants).
- **Safety**: growth factors not associated with risks apart from possible allergy in hypersensitive patients; local effect, no systemic absorption.
- **Cosmeceutical**: Kligman's term, sitting in the unresolved US regulatory gap between cosmetics and APIs.

## Formulation & Practical Guidance
- Use combinations of growth factors and cytokines rather than single factors.
- Expect limited penetration through intact stratum corneum; follicles, sweat glands and compromised skin are the proposed routes — consider delivery and barrier state.
- Support anti-wrinkle and periorbital claims with clinical data (wrinkles, epidermal thickness, dark circles) measured over ≥30 days to ~2–3 months.
- Communicate safety: concentrations in cosmetics are far below levels of concern for VEGF-related tumor promotion; flag allergy risk for hypersensitive users.
- Be precise in labelling "plant stem cells": distinguish cultured undifferentiated-cell extracts from stem (stalk) extracts.
- Ensure benefits are achieved without compromising barrier function.

## Mental Models
- **Aging as an unhealed wound**: supply the missing repair signals rather than adding structural material.
- **Signals work as a network**: growth factors and cytokines regulate each other, so cocktails mirror physiology better than single factors.
- **Dose and penetration define risk**: theoretical hazards (carcinogenicity) need concentrations and uptake far beyond topical reality.

## Anti-patterns
- **Assuming large proteins penetrate intact skin**: they do not easily; claims need delivery rationale.
- **Marketing stem-stalk extracts as "stem cells"**: they contain no undifferentiated cells.
- **Overstating plant stem-cell efficacy**: rigorous, extensive human studies are still lacking.
