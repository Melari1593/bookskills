# Glossary

Key terms from the three books, alphabetical. The file name in parentheses is the chapter to read for depth.

**18-MEA** — 18-methyl eicosanoic acid covalently bound to epicuticle forming the F-layer that gives hair lubricity and hydrophobicity (v1-p3-3-1-hair-physical-chemical-properties)

**2'-O-acetyl-ADP ribose** — unique product of sirtuin deacetylation (v2-p5-5-sirtuins-and-skin)

**3'UTR** — untranslated region downstream of an mRNA's stop codon; miR binding site (v2-p4-3-3-micrornas)

**Absolute** — ethanol extract of a concrete (or supercritical CO2 extract), used at 0.001-0.100% as touch ingredient (v1-p1-2-fragrance-selection)

**Accord / note** — an odor character built from 2-20 materials, like a mini-fragrance (v1-p1-2-fragrance-selection)

**Acquired pellicle** — 1–3 µm salivary protein film that re-forms within minutes of cleaning (v1-p3-6-1-mouth-oral-care)

**Acyl glutamate** — anionic surfactant from glutamic acid acylated with a fatty acid and neutralized; mild to NMF (v2-p4-3-4-amino-acids)

**Adulterated cosmetic** — contains injurious/filthy substance, insanitary manufacture, harmful container or unsafe color additive (v1-p2-2-us-eu-regulation)

**AGE-Reader** — non-invasive autofluorescence device (370 ± 50 nm); readings rise ~0.023 AU/year (v2-p5-4-glycation-proteasome-telomeres)

**AGEs** — advanced glycation end products formed when glucose binds proteins, causing cross-links, yellowing, stiffness (v2-p5-1-theories-of-aging)

**Alpha-2 receptor** — antilipolytic adrenergic receptor, twice as abundant as beta in thigh/buttock fat (v2-p4-2-6-cellulite)

**Alpha-hydroxy acid (AHA)** — carboxylic acid with OH on the alpha carbon; glycolic, lactic, citric, mandelic (v2-p4-3-5-hydroxy-acids)

**Amadori compound** — reversible early glycation product that releases glyoxal, MGO, 3-DG (v2-p5-4-glycation-proteasome-telomeres)

**Amphoteric complex** — AHA complexed with arginine/ornithine for slow release and less sting (v2-p4-3-5-hydroxy-acids)

**AMPK** — three-protein enzyme sensing cellular energy, regulating lipid/glucose metabolism (v1-p1-4-molecular-biology-gene-expression)

**AMPS polymer** — acryloyldimethyltaurate-based cross-linked thickener with pH-independent negative charge (v2-p4-2-2-rheology-modifiers-consumer-perception)

**Anhydrobiosis** — "life without water"; survival of complete desiccation (v2-p5-3-anti-senescence)

**Antagonistic pleiotropy** — one gene with beneficial and detrimental effects; applies to senescence and apoptosis (v2-p5-3-anti-senescence)

**Antioxidant (Halliwell & Gutteridge)** — substance at low concentration vs substrate that significantly delays/prevents its oxidation (v2-p4-3-7-antioxidants)

**Antioxidant paradox** — antioxidants effective in animals but failing in human trials, due to pro-oxidant action and poor delivery (v2-p4-3-2-peptides)

**AP-1** — UV-induced activator protein-1, which induces collagenase and wrinkling (v2-p4-3-4-amino-acids)

**Apnea-Hypopnea Index (AHI)** — obstructive events per sleep hour; >30 severe OSA (v2-p5-8-stress-sleep-epigenetic-orthodontics)

**Apparent permanent shape** — everyday hair shape, a temporary shape in equilibrium with the real permanent shape under weight and fiber interactions (v1-p3-3-4-hair-shape-changes)

**AQP8** — aquaporin 8 ("ammoniaporin"), first found in skin via Salicornia study (v2-p4-2-5-marine-ingredients)

**Aromatase** — enzyme converting testosterone precursors to estrogen; inhibited by chrysin in vitro (v2-p4-2-6-cellulite)

**ASC-CM** — conditioned medium of adipose-derived stem cells; collagen-boosting, MMP-1-lowering, lightening (v2-p4-3-6-growth-factors-stem-cells)

**Assay interference** — nanoparticles distorting in vitro readouts (MTT, LDH, ELISA) (v1-p2-3-4-nanomaterials-regulation)

**Autophagy** — lysosomal "self-eating" clearing long-lived proteins and organelles; backup for proteasome; marker LC3-II (v2-p5-4-glycation-proteasome-telomeres)

**Bacteriocin** — short-chain antimicrobial peptide from Lactobacillus used against acne (v2-p4-1-4-3-fermentation-bioactives)

**Bakuchiol** — photostable meroterpene phenol with retinol-like gene effects; stabilizes retinol (v2-p4-3-7-antioxidants)

**Beau's lines** — transverse depressions recording a period of severe systemic illness (v1-p3-4-nails)

**Beta-1,3-D-glucan** — yeast cell-wall polysaccharide immunostimulant (v2-p4-1-4-2-probiotics)

**Beta glucogallin** — validated biomarker of amla extract (v2-p4-1-5-multifunctional-botanicals)

**Bionic acid** — 3rd-generation PHA: monosaccharide + aldonic acid (lactobionic, maltobionic; ~358 Da) (v2-p4-3-5-hydroxy-acids)

**Bloom** — burst of fragrance when water is added to a rinse-off product; often the key evaluation point (v1-p1-2-fragrance-selection)

**Body (flexural rigidity)** — bundle resistance to bending from fiber density, protein density, inter-fiber friction and temporary shape (v1-p3-3-4-hair-shape-changes)

**Brookfield Yield Value (BYV)** — (η@0.5 rpm − η@1.0 rpm)/100 (v2-p4-2-2-rheology-modifiers-consumer-perception)

**BTH** — bovine testicular hyaluronidase; yields saturated even-numbered HA oligos (v2-p4-1-3-hyaluronic-acid)

**Bulge** — stem cell reservoir in the ORS at the arrector pili insertion, source of all follicle epithelium and reserve melanocytes (v1-p3-3-2-hair-follicle-biology)

**Caloric restriction** — 40–60% calorie cut extends life span in yeast, worms, monkeys via sirtuins; not shown in humans; skin looks older (v2-p5-1-theories-of-aging)

**Canada Hot List** — Canadian list of prohibited/restricted cosmetic ingredients (v2-p4-1-7-1-antimicrobial-preservatives)

**Canities** — hair graying from loss of follicle melanin (v1-p3-3-2-hair-follicle-biology)

**Carnosic acid** — rosemary phenolic diterpene that oxidizes to further active antioxidants (v2-p4-1-7-2-antioxidants-shelf-life)

**Carrier peptide** — peptide that transports trace metals such as Cu²⁺ (e.g., GHK) (v2-p4-3-2-peptides)

**c* (critical overlap concentration)** — polymer concentration where coils overlap and viscosity rises exponentially (v2-p4-2-2-rheology-modifiers-consumer-perception)

**Cell membrane complex (CMC)** — intercellular cement of beta lipid layers and a delta protein layer; diffusion pathway and weakest mechanical link when dry (v1-p3-3-1-hair-physical-chemical-properties)

**Cellular Water Principle** — optimizing intracellular/extracellular water homeostasis as the core of anti-aging (v2-p5-2-cellular-water-principle)

**Ceramide EOS/NP/AH etc.** — letter code: fatty acid N/A/O + base S/P/H, E = ester-linked acid (v1-p3-7-lip-skin)

**CFDA** — China (State) Food & Drug Administration, registers cosmetics (v1-p2-3-3-china)

**Chronic stress** — prolonged overactivation of SNS/HPA axis, not stress intensity (v2-p5-8-stress-sleep-epigenetic-orthodontics)

**Chronic-wound hypothesis** — idea that aging skin behaves like a chronic wound and can be helped by repair signals (v2-p4-3-6-growth-factors-stem-cells)

**Chrono-delivery** — timing product/drug application to circadian windows for better efficacy (v2-p5-7-skin-chronobiology)

**Classification Letter** — official SFDA ruling on a borderline product's category (v1-p2-3-2-saudi-arabia)

**Clay master gel** — bentonite/hectorite pre-dispersed in solvent to control rheology and stop sweating (v2-p4-1-2-lipstick-ingredients)

**CLOCK/BMAL1** — activator heterodimer driving PER/CRY; core of the circadian feedback loop (v2-p5-7-skin-chronobiology)

**Club hair** — fully keratinized dead hair formed during catagen (v1-p3-3-2-hair-follicle-biology)

**CMC** — critical micelle concentration at which micelles start forming (v2-p4-1-1-surfactants)

**CoC (KSA)** — per-shipment Certificate of Conformity from approved inspection body (v1-p2-3-2-saudi-arabia)

**Continuous innovation** — new product requiring almost no consumer behavior change (v1-p1-1-marketing-concepts)

**Corneocyte** — terminally differentiated keratin-filled keratinocyte with cross-linked cornified envelope (v1-p3-1-skin-structure)

**Corneosome** — desmosomal protein junction binding corneocytes (v2-p4-3-5-hydroxy-acids)

**Cosmeceutical** — popular term for active cosmetics; no legal meaning under the FD&C Act (v1-p3-2-4-aging-elderly-skin)

**CpG island** — CpG-rich promoter region; <3% methylated in normal tissue, hypermethylated with age (v2-p5-6-epigenetics-of-skin-aging)

**CPNP** — EU Cosmetic Products Notification Portal; pre-market notification, not authorization (v1-p2-2-us-eu-regulation)

**CPSR** — Cosmetic Product Safety Report: Part A safety information, Part B assessment and reasoning (v1-p2-2-us-eu-regulation)

**CRABP II** — cellular retinoic acid binding protein up-regulated by retinoid-like actives (v2-p4-2-5-marine-ingredients)

**Critical gelation concentration** — level of alkyl silicone at which an oil stops flowing (v2-p4-2-3-1-silicones)

**Critical pH** — ~5.5, below which enamel demineralizes; not an absolute value (v1-p3-6-1-mouth-oral-care)

**Crème gel** — emulsion stabilized by polymeric rheology modifier without traditional emulsifiers (v2-p4-2-2-rheology-modifiers-consumer-perception)

**Crosspolymer** — copolymer linked with a cross-linking agent (v2-p4-2-1-rheological-additives)

**Cup line** — weakest point of a lipstick bullet where over-soft sticks break (v2-p4-1-2-lipstick-ingredients)

**Curcuminoids** — turmeric metabolites that modulate TNFα, NFκB, COX2, IL1 and protect ECM (v2-p4-1-4-1-ayurveda)

**Customs Union (CU)** — Russia, Belarus, Kazakhstan unified customs/technical regulation zone (v1-p2-3-1-russia-customs-union)

**Cuticle buckling** — lifting/deformation of cuticle cells with CMC breakage due to viscoelastic mismatch under tensile or thermal stress (v1-p3-3-1-hair-physical-chemical-properties)

**Cyclomethicone** — cyclic dimethylsiloxanes D4, D5, D6; volatile, dry feel (v2-p4-2-3-1-silicones)

**Cytokeratins** — intermediate filaments forming the keratinocyte cytoskeleton (v2-p4-1-6-skin-barrier-padina)

**Day protect / night repair** — skin uses daytime energy for protection and nighttime for repair, proliferation and regeneration (v2-p5-7-skin-chronobiology)

**Dechloro ethylcloprostenolamide** — synthetic topical prostaglandin analogue for lash enhancement developed in 2007 (v1-p3-3-5-eyelashes)

**Dectin-1** — receptor required for full response to fungal beta-glucans (v2-p4-1-4-2-probiotics)

**Defensins** — epithelial antimicrobial host-defense peptides, also proliferative and anti-inflammatory (v2-p4-1-4-2-probiotics)

**DEJ** — dermal-epidermal junction basement membrane (lamina lucida + lamina densa) (v2-p4-1-6-skin-barrier-padina)

**Demi-permanent color** — ammonia-free, lower-peroxide hair color that is gentler but may not cover >50% gray (v1-p3-3-3-hair-aging)

**Dental calculus** — mineralized plaque, ~80% inorganic, maturing from brushite to apatite (v1-p3-6-1-mouth-oral-care)

**Dental erosion** — acid dissolution of tooth not caused by plaque fermentation (v1-p3-6-1-mouth-oral-care)

**Dermal-epidermal junction (DEJ)** — four-zone basement membrane (laminin 5, collagen IV, collagen VII anchoring fibrils); flattens with age (v2-p4-3-7-antioxidants)

**Dermal papilla** — mesenchymal signaling center in the bulb that dictates hair size, length and anagen duration (v1-p3-3-2-hair-follicle-biology)

**Dermatoglyphic pattern** — surface line network; becomes fewer, deeper, oriented lines with age (v1-p3-2-4-aging-elderly-skin)

**Design patent** — protects nonfunctional ornamental appearance, 14 years (US) (v1-p2-4-intellectual-property)

**Desmosome** — calcium-dependent cell-cell adhesion junction ("press stud") (v2-p4-1-6-skin-barrier-padina)

**Desynchronization** — clock genes out of phase with day–night cycle; linked to cancer, obesity, aging (v2-p5-7-skin-chronobiology)

**DHT** — dihydrotestosterone, the androgen implicated in follicle shrinkage and hair loss (v1-p3-3-3-hair-aging)

**Dimethicone copolyol (PEG/PPG dimethicone)** — silicone with pendant PEG/PPG; surfactant/emulsifier (v2-p4-2-3-1-silicones)

**Discontinuous innovation** — disruptive product creating a new market application, e.g., hair regrowth, ingestible beauty (v1-p1-1-marketing-concepts)

**DNA methylation** — DNMT adds methyl (from SAM) to CpG cytosine, silencing genes (v2-p5-6-epigenetics-of-skin-aging)

**Doctrine of equivalents** — infringement by substantially same function, way and result (v1-p2-4-intellectual-property)

**Dose metric** — unit expressing dose; surface area or particle number may suit nanomaterials better than mass (v1-p2-3-4-nanomaterials-regulation)

**Dosha** — Ayurvedic basic energy type: Vata, Pitta, Kapha (v2-p4-1-4-1-ayurveda)

**Drop point** — relative measure of a stick's melt viscosity, distinct from melt point (v2-p4-1-2-lipstick-ingredients)

**"Drug" (plant)** — dried plant part used for extraction (Dutch *droog*, "dried") (sus-p12-1-sustainability)

**DSL** — Canadian Domestic Substances List; nanoforms with unique structures count as new (v1-p2-3-4-nanomaterials-regulation)

**Dusting** — adding a story ingredient below an efficacious level while still claiming its benefit (v1-p1-1-marketing-concepts)

**Dynamic friction coefficient** — kinetic friction measure (µ) used for hair smoothness (v2-p4-3-4-amino-acids)

**EAC mark** — EurAsian Conformity mark, minimum 5 mm, replaces GOST R (v1-p2-3-1-russia-customs-union)

**Edematous water** — "wasted water" in inflamed tissue (puffiness) unavailable to cells (v2-p5-2-cellular-water-principle)

**Elicitor** — biotic/abiotic stimulus that boosts secondary-metabolite production in plant cell cultures (sus-p12-1-sustainability)

**Emblica** — Phyllanthus emblica tannin extract; cascading, chelating, non-pro-oxidant antioxidant (v2-p4-3-7-antioxidants)

**Endocuticle** — non-cross-linked, negatively charged, water-swellable cuticle layer that fails when hair is wet (v1-p3-3-1-hair-physical-chemical-properties)

**EP Global Skin Classification Scale** — 11-category scale combining race, country of origin, complexion, characteristics, conditions and treatment precautions (v1-p3-2-1-global-skin-classification)

**Epidermal melanin unit** — one melanocyte supplying ~36–40 keratinocytes (v1-p3-1-skin-structure)

**Epigenetic drift** — progressive deregulation of epigenetic marks with age (v2-p5-6-epigenetics-of-skin-aging)

**Epigenetic orthodontics** — functional dental appliances inducing mechano-transduction to remodel craniofacial bone (v2-p5-8-stress-sleep-epigenetic-orthodontics)

**Epigenetics** — heritable changes in gene expression via chromatin, without DNA sequence change (v1-p1-4-molecular-biology-gene-expression)

**Epigenome** — chemical tags on DNA/histones directing which genes are expressed (v2-p5-6-epigenetics-of-skin-aging)

**ERH** — equilibrium relative humidity = aw × 100 (v2-p4-1-7-1-antimicrobial-preservatives)

**Evaluator** — fragrance-house role translating the brief into an olfactive structure and critiquing perfumer trials (v1-p1-2-fragrance-selection)

**Exogen** — active, regulated shedding phase of the club fiber (v1-p3-3-2-hair-follicle-biology)

**Exopolysaccharide (EPS)** — high-MW sugar polymer secreted by microorganisms; unique per species (v2-p4-2-5-marine-ingredients)

**Exosome** — secreted vesicle carrying miRs/proteins between cells (v2-p4-3-3-micrornas)

**Extrinsic aging / photo-aging** — lifestyle-driven aging, chiefly UV, also cigarette smoke (v1-p3-2-4-aging-elderly-skin)

**Fenton reaction** — Fe²⁺ + H₂O₂ → hydroxyl radical (v2-p4-3-7-antioxidants)

**Feughelman two-phase model** — hair as crystalline keratin microfibrils (C phase) in parallel with a water-plasticizable amorphous matrix (M phase) (v1-p3-3-1-hair-physical-chemical-properties)

**Filaggrin** — keratin-aggregating protein from profilaggrin; later degraded into NMF amino acids (v1-p3-1-skin-structure)

**Fitzpatrick scale** — 1975 six-type classification by sunburn/tanning response (v1-p3-2-1-global-skin-classification)

**Flanker** — a variation of an existing fragrance, often distinguished by color (v1-p1-3-fragrance-packaging-design)

**Four P's** — Product, Price, Place, Promotion; the marketing mix tailored to a segment (v1-p1-1-marketing-concepts)

**Four-quadrant system** — subjective/objective × individual/collective model of product perception (v2-p4-1-7-1-antimicrobial-preservatives)

**Fragrance blocker** — ingredient that mutes OR response to a malodor (v1-p3-5-nose-olfaction)

**Fragrance brief** — document giving the fragrance house the olfactive story, categories, oil cost target, benchmarks, regulatory/stability needs (v1-p1-3-fragrance-packaging-design)

**Fragrance enhancer** — ingredient that amplifies OR response to a target note at certain concentrations (v1-p3-5-nose-olfaction)

**Free acid** — undissociated acid fraction, the only immediately bioavailable form; 50% at pH = pKa (v2-p4-3-5-hydroxy-acids)

**Freedom to Operate (FTO)** — country-specific analysis of infringement risk before commercialization (v1-p2-4-intellectual-property)

**Free radical theory** — aging from unpaired-electron molecules damaging membranes, DNA and proteins; Harman 1954; most commonly held theory (v2-p5-1-theories-of-aging)

**Frizz** — fibers out of synchrony with the bundle due to memory damage, cuticle friction or static (v1-p3-3-4-hair-shape-changes)

**Fudo rheometer** — instrument stretching skin/hair at constant rate to break; measures elongation (v2-p4-3-4-amino-acids)

**Gel structure** — hydrophilic, H-bonded part of the amorphous matrix that absorbs water and stores temporary (water-set) shapes (v1-p3-3-1-hair-physical-chemical-properties)

**Gene silencing** — histone deacetylation condenses chromatin and represses genes (v2-p5-5-sirtuins-and-skin)

**Glyoxalase-1** — GSH-dependent enzyme detoxifying glyoxal/MGO; itself damaged by glycation (v2-p5-4-glycation-proteasome-telomeres)

**GOST R** — former Russian conformity certification/mark in the dual system (v1-p2-3-1-russia-customs-union)

**GPMT** — Guinea Pig Maximization Test, only sensitization method accepted by CFDA (v1-p2-3-3-china)

**GRAS fragrance** — every material has a FEMA-GRAS/flavor status; for lip and mouth-contact products (v1-p1-2-fragrance-selection)

**Green Star Rating** — sum over ingredients of Green Star Value × % actives (v2-p4-2-3-1-silicones)

**Growth factor** — secreted protein regulating cell growth and wound repair; immune-regulating ones are cytokines (v2-p4-3-6-growth-factors-stem-cells)

**GSO 1943/2009** — Gulf cosmetic safety standard based on EU 76/768 (v1-p2-3-2-saudi-arabia)

**Gynoid lipodystrophy** — technical name for cellulite (v2-p4-2-6-cellulite)

**Halophyte** — salt-tolerant coastal plant (e.g., Salicornia) (v2-p4-2-5-marine-ingredients)

**HA oligosaccharide** — short fragment (e.g., 6–20 saccharides, <5 kDa) with biological signaling activity (v2-p4-1-3-hyaluronic-acid)

**HASE** — hydrophobically modified alkali-swellable emulsion polymer with 0–3 mol% associative groups (v2-p4-2-2-rheology-modifiers-consumer-perception)

**HAT/HDAC** — enzymes adding/removing histone acetyl groups to open/close chromatin (v2-p5-6-epigenetics-of-skin-aging)

**Hayflick limit** — human fibroblasts divide ~50 times then stop (v2-p5-1-theories-of-aging)

**HFP** — hydrophilic fraction of Padina pavonica, calcium-mobilizing barrier active (v2-p4-1-6-skin-barrier-padina)

**Histone acetylation** — removes histone positive charge, relaxing chromatin and raising transcription (v1-p1-4-molecular-biology-gene-expression)

**HLB** — hydrophilic-lipophilic balance; weight % hydrophilic portion divided by 5 (v2-p4-1-1-surfactants)

**Holocrine** — secretion by whole-cell disintegration, as in sebaceous glands (v1-p3-1-skin-structure)

**Hot topics** — animal-test alternatives, in silico models, nanomaterials, trace impurities, endocrine disruptors, allergens (v1-p2-1-global-regulatory-developments)

**HPA axis** — hypothalamus–pituitary–adrenal stress cascade (CRF → ACTH → cortisol, epinephrine) (v2-p5-8-stress-sleep-epigenetic-orthodontics)

**Human Adipocyte Conditioned Media Extract** — INCI for ADSC-derived growth-factor extract (CTFA, Sept 2006) (v1-p3-8-feminine-rejuvenation)

**Hyaluronan (HA)** — linear polysaccharide of glucuronic acid and N-acetylglucosamine; holds up to 1000× its weight in water (v2-p4-1-3-hyaluronic-acid)

**Hydrodynamic theory** — tubular fluid movement in exposed dentin stimulates pulpal nerves, causing sensitivity (v1-p3-6-1-mouth-oral-care)

**Hydrodynamic volume** — effective volume a polymer occupies in solvent; larger means higher viscosity (v2-p4-2-2-rheology-modifiers-consumer-perception)

**Hyponychium** — distal seal between fingertip skin and plate that protects the bed (v1-p3-4-nails)

**ICCR** — International Cooperation on Cosmetics Regulation: regulators of Canada, EU, Japan, US pursuing convergence (v1-p2-1-global-regulatory-developments)

**IECIC** — Inventory of Existing Cosmetic Ingredients in China (v1-p2-3-3-china)

**INCI blend** — fragrance whose every component has an INCI name so the label lists them instead of "fragrance" (v1-p1-2-fragrance-selection)

**Induction period (IP)** — OSI time to rapid oxidation (v2-p4-1-7-2-antioxidants-shelf-life)

**Inflammasome** — multiprotein complex activated by UV in keratinocytes, driving inflammation (v1-p1-4-molecular-biology-gene-expression)

**Inflammation theory** — ROS → PLA2 → arachidonic acid → inflammatory mediators → more ROS, a self-perpetuating loop (v2-p5-1-theories-of-aging)

**Inner root sheath** — Henle, Huxley and IRS cuticle layers that anchor, mold and guide the growing shaft (v1-p3-3-2-hair-follicle-biology)

**In-store intercept** — qualitative method engaging shoppers in-store to test scents (v1-p1-3-fragrance-packaging-design)

**Integrin** — cell-surface receptor mediating ECM–cell signalling (v2-p4-3-2-peptides)

**Intended use** — basis of US cosmetic/drug classification, from claims, consumer perception and known actives (v1-p2-2-us-eu-regulation)

**Interstitial pore volume** — space in the elastomer network set by cross-link density; holds solvent, sebum or actives (v2-p4-2-3-2-silicone-elastomers)

**Intracellular water (ICW)** — cytosolic water; must be steady for metabolism and youthful look; falls with age (v2-p5-2-cellular-water-principle)

**Intrinsic aging** — time-dependent aging on all sites, independent of lifestyle (v1-p3-2-4-aging-elderly-skin)

**Intrinsic vs extrinsic hair aging** — genetic/hormonal aging (graying, alopecia) vs environmental damage (UV, pollution, grooming, chemicals) (v1-p3-3-3-hair-aging)

**Keloid** — large raised scar from abnormal healing, common risk in darker skin (v1-p3-2-1-global-skin-classification)

**Kenogen** — interval when the follicle is empty after shedding and before new anagen; longer in androgenetic alopecia (v1-p3-3-2-hair-follicle-biology)

**Keratolytic** — agent loosening the horny layer so dead cells shed (v2-p4-3-4-amino-acids)

**Koebnerization** — appearance of lesions at sites of trauma (v1-p3-2-2-skin-of-color-disorders)

**Krafft temperature** — temperature above which lipophilic emulsifiers in water form lamellar liquid crystals (v2-p4-1-1-surfactants)

**Kraurosis vulvae** — end-stage sclerosing atrophy with stenosis and effacement of labia minora/clitoris (v1-p3-8-feminine-rejuvenation)

**Label patch** — Chinese-language sticker over original label, reviewed for claims (v1-p2-3-3-china)

**Lamellar body** — 0.2–0.5 µm Golgi-derived secretory organelle delivering barrier lipids, enzymes and AMPs at the granulosum–SC interface (v1-p3-1-skin-structure)

**Langerhans cell** — epidermal antigen-presenting dendritic cell with Birbeck granules and langerin; UVB-impaired (v1-p3-1-skin-structure)

**Lanthionine** — monosulfide cross-link formed from cystine by OH- in relaxers, proposed to lock the straight shape (v1-p3-3-4-hair-shape-changes)

**Lash conditioner** — topical product that increases lash length/thickness by shifting follicles into a longer anagen (v1-p3-3-5-eyelashes)

**Light interference patterns (LIPs)** — iridescent colors from air-filled lifted cuticle layers, an optical marker of cuticle damage (v1-p3-3-1-hair-physical-chemical-properties)

**Lipofuscin** — yellow-brown metabolic "garbage" pigment; excess shows as aging spots (v2-p5-1-theories-of-aging)

**log Kow** — octanol-water partition coefficient; per unit, ~1,000x more material prefers the oil phase (as stated) (v1-p1-2-fragrance-selection)

**Lunula** — visible half-moon of the matrix, probably showing incomplete keratinization (v1-p3-4-nails)

**LYCD** — live yeast cell derivative from S. cerevisiae; tissue respiration and wound healing (v2-p4-1-4-3-fermentation-bioactives)

**Lye / no-lye / base / no-base / mix relaxers** — NaOH vs other hydroxides; separate vs built-in petrolatum; two-part guanidine generation (v1-p3-3-4-hair-shape-changes)

**Lysate / ferment** — INCI descriptors for probiotic-derived ingredients (v2-p4-1-4-2-probiotics)

**MAC / MUCAP** — maximum approved concentration / maximum used concentration in approved products (v1-p2-3-3-china)

**Marangoni effect** — foam collapse via film drainage below critical thickness (v2-p4-1-1-surfactants)
