# Cheatsheet

## Decision Tree: Which Condition Chapter?
1. **Can't fall/stay asleep, but mood is otherwise OK?** → Ch 4 Insomnia (check Deficient Vexation vs. heat vs. Heart-Kidney pattern first)
2. **Energy is exhausted, "stuck," hopeless, low mood?** → Ch 5 Depression (Yu/stagnation family)
3. **Energy is rushing, uncontrolled, panicky, worried?** → Ch 6 Anxiety (Excess vs. Deficient pattern first)
4. **Inattention/hyperactivity/impulsivity, especially in a school/work context?** → Ch 7 ADHD (identify yin/inattentive vs. yang/hyperactive pole)
5. **Clouded thinking, memory/focus loss, often post-viral or chronic-illness-linked?** → Ch 8 Brain Fog (check Spleen/Kidney/Heart)
6. **Symptoms trace to a specific overwhelming event, or physical symptoms with no clear cause?** → Ch 9 Trauma (check for Heart Closed/Stuck Qi in Chest)
- Symptoms rarely sit in only one chapter — insomnia, depression, anxiety, and trauma share extensive pattern/formula overlap; check adjacent chapters when the first doesn't fully resolve symptoms.

## Decision Rule: Depression vs. Anxiety
"When one feels stuck with exhausted energy → depression. When one feels a rush of uncontrolled energy → anxiety." Same underlying stagnation family, opposite energetic direction. If unsure whether a racing-heart sensation is anxiety or excitement, check for a plausible non-threatening trigger (e.g., new relationship) before assuming pathology.

## Decision Rule: Formula Not Working?
If no noticeable improvement within **1–2 weeks** at full dosage → wrong pattern match, not a dosage problem. Re-examine physical symptoms (temperature of hands/feet, digestion, pulse quality) to re-diagnose the pattern rather than increasing dose or waiting longer.

## Decision Rule: First-Line Anxiety Formulas When Pattern Is Unclear
Try, in order of general safety/availability: **Jia Wei Xiao Yao San/Wan → An Shen Ding Zhi Wan → Gui Pi Tang.** All three are widely available with low side-effect profiles.

## Tells & Smells
- **Cold hands/feet + irritability/stuck feeling** → Liver Qi Stagnation family (Si Ni San, Xiao Yao San) — don't mistake for a purely circulatory issue.
- **"Wired but tired" + racing thoughts, dark circles** → severe Heart-Kidney noninteraction insomnia pattern, not simple Deficient Vexation.
- **Digestive symptoms (loose stools, bloating) accompanying a mental health complaint** → check Spleen involvement / Food Stagnation / Internal Dampness before treating the mental symptom alone.
- **Sudden rushing sensation abdomen→chest with panic** → Running Piglet (Ben Tun), not generic anxiety — needs Gui Zhi + Fu Ling-based formulas specifically.
- **Chest oppression/tightness with disrupted heart rhythm** → possible Heart Closed / Stuck Qi in Chest (trauma pattern) — check PC 6 Nei Guan responsiveness.
- **Symptoms worse with stress specifically** → Liver Qi Stagnation signature — favors the Xiao Yao San family of formulas.
- **Physical symptoms (sweating, palpitations, racing breath) with no clear precipitating cause** → possible underlying unaddressed trauma, per Shanghanlun.
