# Chapter 6: Anxiety

## Core Idea
Anxiety is the TCM condition with the most granular pattern differentiation — instead of one sedating approach, TCM names distinct symptom-clusters (vexation/fan, Running Piglet/Ben Tun, agitation/zao, Blood Deficiency) each mapped to specific formulas, letting treatment target the patient's exact physiological signature rather than "anxiety" as a monolith.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Excess vs. Deficient pattern split** (the most basic anxiety differentiation):
  - **Excess** = qi/Blood stagnation: "stuckness," pain below the ribs, neck tension, irritability, headaches (Qi Stagnation); or fixed/stabbing pain, menstrual clots, labile emotions (Blood stasis).
  - **Deficient** = vexation-type patterns and Running Piglet — a felt "loss" or inability to face the unknown (per Dr. Leon Hammer).
- **Five Phases anxiety triggers** (after Dr. Leon Hammer's *Dragon Rises, Red Bird Flies*):
  | Phase | Organ | Trigger | Sample Symptoms |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Water | Kidney | Adverse early-life experience, fear of unknown, constitutional factors | Chronic fatigue, fearfulness, low back pain, somnolence |
  | Wood | Liver | Advance/retreat conflict, stress, opposition | Qi Stagnation, irritability, perfectionism, fear of humiliation |
  | Fire | Heart/Pericardium | Self-expression, communication, love, romance | Palpitations, Running Piglet, social anxiety, fear of public speaking |
  | Earth | Spleen | Bonding, compassion, self-care, unmet needs | Digestive issues, heavy/tired feeling, loss of a caregiver |
  | Metal | Lung | Growth, autonomy, change, grief, loss | Shortness of breath, shallow breathing, fear of change, school phobia |
- **Vexation (fan, 煩) vs. Running Piglet (Ben Tun, 奔豚)**: Vexation = "fire next to the head" — heat-driven mental irritability/restlessness. Running Piglet = a sudden rushing sensation from abdomen to chest/throat with panic — Kidney Water resources mobilized upward/outward under fright, resembling sympathetic hyperarousal (fight-or-flight).
  - When to use: distinguishing a "hot, irritable" anxiety presentation (vexation) from a "sudden panic surge" presentation (Running Piglet) determines formula family.
- **Two core herb pairs used across anxiety formulas**:
  - **Gui Zhi (cinnamon) + Fu Ling (poria)**: counteracts "counterflow" (excess upward/outward energy) while calming spirit and strengthening the center — base of most Running Piglet/anxiety formulas.
  - **Long Gu (dragon bone) + Mu Li (oyster shell)** — "Heavy Spirit Settling" minerals: Mu Li (salty) descends into Water phase to dissolve obstruction; Long Gu (bland) grounds and stabilizes.

## Key Concepts
- **Zao (躁, agitation)**: A more physical restlessness than vexation, usually heat-derived (fever, overwork, chronic inflammation).
- **Calming the spirit (安神, an shen)**: The core therapeutic principle for all anxiety and panic treatment in TCM.
- **Anxiety/anger/joy triad** (Suwen): joy overcomes anxiety; anxiety overcomes anger; anger is overcome by joy — a classical emotion-regulation cycle physicians used to justify joy-generating interventions for anxiety.
- **First-line formula picks**: An Shen Ding Zhi Wan, Gui Pi Tang, or Jia Wei Xiao Yao San/Wan — widely available, low side-effect profile, good starting points when unsure of exact pattern. Jia Wei Xiao Yao San/Wan alone comprises an estimated 33–45% of all Chinese medicine formulas sold.

## Mental Models
- Excitement and anxiety can be physiologically indistinguishable (e.g., heart palpitations from a new romantic relationship vs. panic) — always clarify context/trigger before assuming pathology.
- Anxiety, depression, and insomnia share overlapping patterns and formulas — treat the three chapters as one interconnected diagnostic space, not silos.

## Anti-patterns
- **Reaching for a single sedating approach**: Misses that different anxiety presentations (heat/vexation vs. panic/Running Piglet vs. Blood Deficiency) need structurally different formulas.
- **High caffeine intake when anxiety-prone**: Clinical studies show caffeine equivalent to 5 cups of coffee can trigger panic attacks in panic-disorder patients; tea's lower caffeine (~1/4–1/3 of coffee) plus L-theanine and GABA content instead promotes "calm wakefulness."
- **Judging a formula before 1–2 weeks**: If no noticeable difference within the first 1–2 weeks at full dosage, it's likely the wrong formula for the pattern.

## Worked Example
**Acupressure sequence for anxiety** (press a few minutes each; finger, acupressure stick, or pencil eraser — no need for hard pressure):
1. **Auricular massage**: warm hands, massage ear front-to-back, gently pull posterior ear forward 5–10x, massage from apex to earlobe with thumb/index finger until warm, then gently pull the earlobe down/out 5–10x.
2. **Sanyinjiao (SP 6)**: a hand's width above the inner ankle bone (medial malleolus) — gentle pressure until soreness subsides; calms spirit, also eases menstrual cramps.
3. **Heart Meridian (HT 7 Shenmen, "Spirit Gate")**: press along the palmar wrist crease toward the palm/little finger — Dr. Leon Hammer called these "hypnotic points" for their calming effect.
4. **LV 3 Tai Chong**: between the 1st/2nd metatarsals on top of the foot — small rotating pressure for a few minutes; also studied for reducing hypertension; use especially if flank/rib pain accompanies stress.

**Five Element music protocol** (from a 2018 Beijing Normal University controlled study on anxiety-with-depression, more effective than the pharmaceutical-only control group): patient-selected tracks, played in a quiet, comfortable, sunlit room at 9 a.m. and 3 p.m., 30 minutes each, for 4 weeks. Gong-note tracks (e.g., "Shimian Maifu," "Good Night," "Idle Life") suit anxiety specifically; suitable keys overall are Gong (Spleen/Earth), Zhi (Heart/Fire), Jue (Liver/Wood), Yu (Kidney/Water) — steady rhythms, avoiding dramatic ups/downs.

**Pu'er/oolong tea for anxiety**: GABA-rich teas (white, oolong, raw pu'er) cross the blood-brain barrier and reduce stress scores; ripe pu'er steeps 1 minute, raw pu'er 30–45 seconds, increasing steep time 15–30 sec per re-steep; discard leaves at day's end.

## Key Takeaways
1. Diagnose by pattern family first (Excess/Qi-Blood stagnation vs. Deficient/vexation-Running Piglet) before picking a formula.
2. Jia Wei Xiao Yao San/Wan, An Shen Ding Zhi Wan, and Gui Pi Tang are safe, well-tolerated first-line choices when the exact pattern is unclear.
3. Gui Zhi + Fu Ling and Long Gu + Mu Li are the two herb-pair "engines" underlying most Running Piglet/anxiety formulas — recognizing them helps predict a formula's action even without full ingredient lookup.
4. Tea (especially GABA-enriched oolong/pu'er) is a well-evidenced anxiety adjunct; high-dose coffee/caffeine is a documented anxiety trigger.
5. Five Element music (Gong/Zhi/Jue/Yu keys, steady rhythm, 30 min 2x/day) has two controlled studies supporting efficacy for anxiety, including outperforming pharmaceutical-only treatment for anxiety-with-depression.
