# Glossary — Fluid Mechanics (Bar-Meir)

**Absolute (dynamic) viscosity, μ** — proportionality constant between shear stress and velocity gradient, τ=μ(dU/dy); units N·sec/m² (Ch1)
**Angular momentum equation** — moment-of-momentum RTT applied to r×U; the torque/shaft-power tool for turbomachinery (Ch6)
**Average velocity (momentum vs. energy)** — momentum-averaged U_avg²=(1/A)∫U²dA differs from energy-averaged (via C_F); never interchange (Ch6, Ch7)
**Body force** — force acting through the volume/mass (gravity); vs. surface force (Ch3, Ch6)
**Boundary velocity (U_b, U_bn)** — velocity of a deformable control volume's own moving surface (Ch5)
**Bulk modulus (B_T)** — ρ·dP/dρ; resistance to compression; used for deep-liquid density-depth relations (Ch1, Ch4)
**Buckingham π theorem** — n parameters spanning i basic dimensions reduce to n−i independent dimensionless groups (Ch9)
**Capillary number (Ca)** — μU/σ = We/Re; viscous vs. surface-tension forces (Ch9)
**Center of mass / centroid** — mass-weighted average position, coordinate-independent (Ch3)
**Choking** — flow reaches a Mach-number ceiling (M=1, or M=1/√k isothermal) beyond which downstream changes can't propagate upstream (Ch11, Ch13)
**Circulation (Γ)** — closed-loop line integral of U·ds; drives lift via Kutta-Joukowski (Ch10)
**Compressibility factor (Z)** — PV/RT; real-gas correction to ideal gas law (Ch2, Ch4)
**Control volume (c.v.)** — fixed (or moving) region of space through which mass/momentum/energy may flow; Eulerian counterpart to a system (Ch5)
**Convective acceleration** — (U·grad)U; nonzero even at steady state (e.g., nozzle flow) (Ch8)
**Detached shock** — occurs when required deflection exceeds δ_max; shock stands off the body (Ch12)
**Doublet (dipole)** — limiting source+sink pair; combined with uniform flow gives cylinder flow (Ch10)
**Effective gravity (g_eff)** — true gravity plus negative of container acceleration; governs all hydrostatic problems (Ch4)
**Energy loss (extended Bernoulli)** — lumped irreversible dissipation term, ∝U², added to Bernoulli for real systems (Ch7)
**Enthalpy (h)** — h=E_u+P/ρ (or PV); bundles internal energy with flow work (Ch2, Ch7)
**Entropy (S)** — defined via ds≡(δQ/T)_rev; state property from Clausius equality (Ch2)
**Euler number (Eu)** — ΔP/(½ρU²); pressure vs. inertial forces = pressure coefficient C_p (Ch9)
**Fanno flow** — adiabatic, constant-area duct flow with friction; friction alone drives M→1 (Ch11)
**First law of thermodynamics** — Q₁₂−W₁₂=E₂−E₁; path-independent energy bookkeeping (Ch2)
**Flooding** — counter-current gas/liquid flow reversal when interfacial shear exceeds critical value (Ch13)
**Flow regime (multiphase)** — qualitatively distinct phase configuration (stratified, slug, annular, bubbly, mist, etc.) (Ch13)
**Fluid Static (Hydrostatic) Equation** — grad P = ρ·g_eff; master equation of Ch4 (Ch4)
**Fourier's law** — dq̇=k_T(dT/dn)dA; heat-conduction model used in the energy equation (Ch7)
**Fr (Froude number)** — U²/(gℓ); inertial vs. gravitational forces (Ch9)
**Gibbs equation (Tds relation)** — Tds=du+Pdv=dh−dP/ρ; links entropy to internal energy/enthalpy (Ch2)
**Homogeneous flow model** — treats a 2-phase mixture as one pseudo-fluid with averaged ρ, μ (Ch13)
**Hydraulic diameter (D_H)** — 4×area/wetted-perimeter; generalizes pipe relations to non-circular ducts (Ch11)
**Ideal gas law** — P=ρRT, R=R̄/M (Ch2)
**Impulse function (F)** — PA(1+kM²); Mach-only bookkeeping for net duct/nozzle force (Ch11)
**Irrotational / potential flow** — curl(U)=0; velocity field is grad of a scalar potential φ (Ch10)
**Isentropic process** — reversible + adiabatic ⟹ dS=0 (not the converse) (Ch2, Ch11)
**Kinematic viscosity (ν)** — μ/ρ, units m²/sec (Ch1)
**Kutta-Joukowski theorem** — Lift L=−ρ∞U∞Γ per unit span (Ch10)
**Lagrangian vs. Eulerian** — tracking a fixed mass (system) vs. a fixed region of space (control volume) (Ch5)
**Lockhart-Martinelli model** — separated-flow correlation via two-phase multipliers φ_G, Ξ (Ch13)
**Mach angle (μ)** — sin⁻¹(1/M); direction of Mach/characteristic lines (Ch12)
**Mach number (M)** — U/c; the master switch for compressibility effects (Ch9, Ch11)
**Metacentric height (GM)** — GM=BM−BG; floating-body stability requires GM>0 (Ch4)
**Moment of inertia** — ∫r²dm (or dA); axis-dependent, unlike center of mass (Ch3)
**Navier-Stokes equations** — ρDU/Dt=−grad P+μ∇²U+f_B (incompressible form); governing PDE of viscous flow (Ch8)
**Newtonian fluid** — τ_xy=μ(dU/dy) with constant μ (Ch1)
**No-slip condition** — fluid velocity equals boundary velocity at a solid wall (valid at macro scale) (Ch5, Ch8)
**Normal shock** — irreversible supersonic→subsonic jump satisfying mass/momentum/energy but not entropy (Ch11)
**Oblique shock** — shock inclined at angle θ; only the normal velocity component "sees" a normal-shock jump (Ch12)
**Parallel Axis Theorem** — I_x'x'=I_xx+r²A (Ch3)
**Piezometric pressure** — P(h)−P0=ρgh for constant-density fluid (Ch4)
**Prandtl-Meyer expansion** — isentropic fan of Mach waves turning supersonic flow away from itself, governed by ν(M) (Ch12)
**Product of inertia** — ∫xy dA; signed, zero for any symmetry axis (Ch3)
**Quality (X)** — gas mass fraction in two-phase flow, X=ṁ_G/ṁ (Ch13)
**Rankine-Hugoniot relations** — the algebraic normal-shock jump conditions (Ch11)
**Rayleigh flow** — frictionless, constant-area duct flow with heat addition; heat alone drives M→1 (Ch11)
**Rayleigh-Taylor instability** — heavy-over-light interface instability; stable only below critical wavelength L_c (Ch4)
**Reiner-Phillippoff model** — non-Newtonian mixture viscosity correlation (Ch1)
**Reynolds number (Re)** — ρUℓ/μ; inertial vs. viscous forces (Ch9)
**Reynolds Transport Theorem (RTT)** — D/Dt∫_sys(fρ)dV = d/dt∫_cv(fρ)dV + ∫_S fρU_rn dA; the master template for every conservation law in the book (Ch5, Ch6, Ch7, Ch8)
**Shaft work** — mechanical work through a shaft (pump adds, turbine extracts) (Ch7)
**Shear work** — work by tangential/viscous stress at the control surface (Ch7)
**Slip line** — interface downstream of asymmetric shocks/expansions; static pressure and direction match, stagnation pressure need not (Ch12)
**Speed of sound (c)** — √(dP/dρ)|s = √(kRT) for ideal gas (Ch11)
**Stagnation (total) state** — hypothetical fully-decelerated isentropic state, subscript 0 (Ch11)
**Star (*) condition** — reference state at M=1; ratios to it depend only on k (Ch11)
**Stream function (ψ)** — Ux=∂ψ/∂y, Uy=−∂ψ/∂x; automatically satisfies 2D continuity (Ch10)
**Substantial (material) derivative** — D/Dt = ∂/∂t + U·grad (Ch8)
**Superficial velocity** — velocity a phase would have if it alone filled the duct (Ch13)
**Surface tension (σ)** — force per unit length at an interface; drives capillary rise, stabilizes short-wavelength interfaces (Ch1, Ch4)
**Sutherland's equation** — gas viscosity vs. temperature correlation (Ch1)
**System (thermo/mechanics)** — fixed quantity of mass tracked as it moves (Lagrangian) (Ch5)
**Vorticity (Ω)** — curl(U); its vanishing defines irrotational flow (Ch10)
**Weber number (We)** — ρU²ℓ/σ; inertial vs. surface-tension forces (Ch9)
