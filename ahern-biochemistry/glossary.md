# Glossary

**A-, B-, Z-DNA** — the three DNA helix conformations: B (standard, right-handed, 10 bp/turn), A (dehydrated/RNA-hybrid, right-handed, 11 bp/turn), Z (left-handed zigzag, alternating purine-pyrimidine tracts) (Ch4).
**Aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase (aaRS)** — Class I/II enzyme family that charges tRNA with its cognate amino acid via a two-step (activation, transfer) mechanism (Ch11).
**Activation energy** — the energy barrier to reach the transition state; enzymes lower it without changing ΔG or Keq (Ch6).
**Alarmone (pppGpp)** — stress-signal nucleotide triggering the bacterial stringent response during amino-acid starvation (Ch13).
**Allosteric site** — a regulatory binding site topographically distinct from the active (orthosteric) site (Ch8).
**Alpha helix** — right-handed secondary structure, 3.6 residues/turn, i→i+4 H-bonds (Ch2).
**Alternative polyadenylation (APA)** — use of different poly-A signal sites, altering 3'UTR content; affects ~70% of the human transcriptome (Ch10).
**Alternative splicing** — regulated exon inclusion/exclusion via ESE/ESS/ISE/ISS elements, expanding proteome diversity (Ch10).
**AP (abasic) site** — a base-lacking DNA position, ~10,000 apurinic + ~500 apyrimidinic sites/cell/day; BER intermediate (Ch12).
**Anfinsen's Dogma** — native 3D structure is fully and thermodynamically encoded by primary sequence (Ch2).
**Apoenzyme / holoenzyme** — enzyme lacking vs. bound to its required cofactor (Ch6, Ch8).
**Attenuation** — RNA-structure-based premature-termination control (trp operon leader) exploiting coupled bacterial transcription-translation (Ch13).
**Base Excision Repair (BER)** — glycosylase-initiated repair of single damaged bases (short- or long-patch) (Ch12).
**Catabolite repression / CAP-cAMP** — glucose-dependent AND-gate control ensuring bacteria use glucose before lactose (Ch13).
**Chromatin remodeling complexes** — ISWI/CHD (spacing), SWI/SNF (ejection), INO80 (histone exchange) — ATP-dependent nucleosome repositioning (Ch13).
**Chromosome territory (CT)** — the discrete nuclear volume each interphase chromosome occupies; competing CT-IC, ICN, Fraser-Bickmore models (Ch4).
**Codon / reading frame / wobble** — triplet genetic code (64 codons, 3 stop); wobble pairing at the 3rd position lets fewer tRNAs read all codons (Ch1, Ch11).
**Cofactor / coenzyme / prosthetic group** — non-protein catalytic requirement; coenzyme = organic; prosthetic group = tightly bound (Ch6, Ch7).
**Cryo-EM** — structure method needing least sample, no crystal, resolves very large complexes near-atomically but not dynamics (Ch3).
**DNA damage checkpoints** — ATM/ATR→CHK1/CHK2→p53/CDK-inhibition cascade arresting the cell cycle for repair (Ch12).
**DNA gyrase / topoisomerase I/II/IV** — Type II (ATP-dependent, both strands: gyrase relieves +supercoils, IV decatenates) vs Type I (one strand, no ATP, relieves −supercoils only) (Ch9).
**Edman degradation** — sequential N-terminal protein sequencing, ~30–60 residue limit (Ch3).
**Elongation factors** — EF-Tu/eEF1α (aa-tRNA delivery), EF-G/eEF2 (translocation) (Ch11).
**End-replication problem** — inability to replace the terminal RNA primer at a linear chromosome's 3' end; solved by telomerase (Ch4, Ch9).
**Enzyme Commission (EC) number** — 4-part classification; 1st digit = oxidoreductase/transferase/hydrolase/lyase/isomerase/ligase (Ch6).
**Epigenome / epigenetics** — heritable (across mitoses, contested across generations) gene-regulatory state not encoded in DNA sequence itself (Ch1, Ch13).
**FPLC vs HPLC** — FPLC preserves native protein conformation (preparative purification); HPLC's pressure/solvents often denature (Ch3).
**G-quadruplex / T-loop** — telomere-protective single-stranded DNA structures (Ch4).
**Gibbs free energy (ΔG)** — ΔG = ΔG° + RTlnQ; ΔG<0 spontaneous toward products, =0 equilibrium, >0 toward reactants (Ch1).
**Henderson-Hasselbalch equation** — pH = pKa + log([A⁻]/[HA]); buffering strongest at pH = pKa (Ch1).
**Histone code** — combinatorial PTM pattern on histones (acetylation→open, methylation→variable) read by writer/eraser/reader proteins (Ch13).
**Homologous Recombination (HR)** — high-fidelity DSB repair using sister chromatid (BRCA1/2, Rad51); restricted to S/G2/M (Ch12).
**Induced fit** — substrate binding reshapes the enzyme active site (modern model, vs. rigid lock-and-key) (Ch1, Ch6).
**Intrinsically disordered protein (IDP)** — lacks fixed 3D structure; often folds upon binding a partner (Ch2).
**Isozymes vs. allozymes** — isozymes = different genes, same reaction; allozymes = allelic variants of one gene (Ch7, Ch8).
**Kinetic proofreading** — two-step (initial selection + proofreading) ribosomal discrimination beyond simple binding thermodynamics (Ch11).
**Km / Vmax** — Michaelis constant (inverse affinity, [S] at ½Vmax) and maximal rate at saturation (Ch6).
**Le Chatelier's Principle** — a perturbed equilibrium shifts to relieve the perturbation (Ch1).
**Lineweaver-Burk plot** — 1/v vs 1/[S] linearization of Michaelis-Menten; diagnoses inhibitor type by intercept pattern (Ch6).
**Meselson-Stahl experiment** — density-labeling proof of semiconservative DNA replication (Ch9).
**Michaelis-Menten kinetics** — v = Vmax[S]/(Km+[S]), the default enzyme rate model (Ch6).
**Mismatch Repair (MMR)** — strand-discriminated correction of replication mismatches; failure linked to ~90% of HNPCC (Ch12).
**Molecular chaperone** — assists folding kinetically (prevents aggregation) without encoding structure itself (Ch2).
**MWC (concerted) vs. KNF (sequential) models** — allosteric regulation: obligatory whole-oligomer T⇌R switch vs. graded per-subunit induced fit (Ch6, Ch8).
**Nonhomologous End-Joining (NHEJ)** — cell-cycle-independent, lower-fidelity DSB repair (Ku70/80, DNA-PKcs, Ligase IV) (Ch12).
**Nucleosome** — ~146 bp DNA wrapped 1.65× around a histone (H2A/H2B/H3/H4) octamer (Ch4, Ch13).
**Nucleotide Excision Repair (NER)** — repairasome-based excision of bulky/helix-distorting lesions; GG-NER (genome-wide) vs. TC-NER (transcribed strand) (Ch12).
**Operon** — cluster of genes under one promoter/operator, polycistronic in bacteria; repressible (trp) vs. inducible (lac) (Ch13).
**Oxyanion hole** — backbone-amide electrostatic clamp stabilizing tetrahedral transition-state intermediates (proteases, ribozymes) (Ch7).
**p53** — stress-activated "guardian of the genome" transcription factor stabilized by PTMs, driving arrest/repair/apoptosis (Ch13).
**Peptide bond** — planar, non-rotating amide linkage (resonance) between amino acids (Ch2).
**Peptidyl transferase center (PTC)** — rRNA-based (ribozyme) catalytic core of the large ribosomal subunit (Ch11).
**pI (isoelectric point)** — pH of zero net charge; used in IEF and 2-DE separation (Ch2, Ch3).
**PIC (Preinitiation Complex)** — >100-protein Pol II assembly (TFIIA/B/D/E/F/H) required before eukaryotic transcription starts (Ch10).
**Post-translational modification (PTM)** — covalent modification after translation (phosphorylation, acetylation, ubiquitination, etc.) expanding the proteome >10-fold beyond gene count (Ch8).
**Proteasome (26S)** — 20S core (proteolytic) + 19S regulatory particle (deubiquitinates/unfolds/translocates) (Ch8).
**Ramachandran plot** — Phi/Psi torsion-angle map of sterically allowed backbone conformations (Ch2).
**Replisome** — multi-protein DNA-synthesis machine (helicase, primase, polymerase, clamp) at the replication fork (Ch9).
**Restriction endonuclease** — sequence-specific DNA-cutting enzyme (EcoRI, EcoRV, etc.); paired with methyltransferase for host protection (Ch5, Ch7).
**Ribozyme** — catalytic RNA (spliceosome core, peptidyl transferase center); supports the RNA World hypothesis (Ch7, Ch10, Ch11).
**Rho factor** — hexameric ATP-dependent helicase mediating factor-dependent bacterial transcription termination (Ch10).
**Semiconservative replication** — each daughter duplex retains one parental strand (Meselson-Stahl) (Ch9).
**Serine protease catalytic triad** — Ser-His-Asp mechanism (chymotrypsin, trypsin, subtilisin via convergent evolution) (Ch7).
**Sigma factor (σ)** — dissociable RNAP subunit conferring bacterial promoter specificity (σ⁷⁰ housekeeping, σ³⁸ stress) (Ch10).
**Splicing / spliceosome** — E→A→B→B*→C complex assembly removing introns via two transesterifications (Ch10).
**Telomerase** — TERT (reverse transcriptase) + TERC (RNA template) extending chromosome 3' ends; restricted in most somatic cells (Ch4, Ch9).
**Translesion synthesis (TLS)** — low-fidelity polymerase bypass of unrepaired lesions to prevent fork collapse (Ch12).
**Ubiquitin-proteasome system** — E1→E2→E3 conjugation cascade tagging substrates (Lys48-polyUb) for 26S degradation (Ch8).
**Watson-Crick base pairing** — A-T (2 H-bonds), G-C (3 H-bonds); antiparallel 5'→3'/3'→5' strands (Ch4).
**Wobble hypothesis** — non-Watson-Crick pairing at the anticodon's first position permits one tRNA to read multiple codons (Ch11).
**Zymogen** — inactive enzyme precursor (trypsinogen, procaspases) requiring proteolytic activation (Ch8).
