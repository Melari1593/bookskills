It does not judge culinary parameters (Brix targets, cut sizes, oven temperatures for texture); those are technique, not testable health claims. When answering from this skill, apply the notes below whenever one of these items comes up: give the book's method and the safety note together.

## Allergen note

The book uses peanuts, tree nuts (almond, hazelnut, pistachio, walnut, pecan, cashew, macadamia, Brazil nut), sesame, egg, milk, wheat and soy throughout. When adapting a recipe for service, list these on the menu. Not a claim of the book, so not scored above.

## Gaps

- No pH data for any preserve in the book; the boiling-water-bath advice in S4 assumes typical fruit pH below 4.6.
- No portion threshold for lychee toxins (S7) or for Brazil-nut selenium in adults.
- Oyster poaching (S5): the recipe's texture target and the safety target cannot both be met; the choice is the cook's.
