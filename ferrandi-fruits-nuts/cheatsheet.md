# Cheatsheet — Fruits & Nuts Quick Reference

Compact decision rules and numbers pulled from across the book. Keep this open while cooking.

## Sugar Stage / Brix Targets (by preparation)

| Preparation | Target | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Sorbet base | 27-30° Brix | Below = hard/icy; above = won't freeze right |
| Watermelon granita | 17° Brix | Lower sugar than sorbet — needs to stay scrapeable |
| Citrus marmalade | 63° Brix, cook to 223°F/106°C | 4x fresh-water blanch first to cut bitterness |
| Quince jelly | 70° Brix, cook to 223-224°F/106-107°C | Juice : sugar = 1:1 by weight |
| Passion fruit/apricot pâte de fruits | 73-74° Brix, cook to 223-225°F/106-107°C | Dry cut pieces 12h before sugar-coating |
| Strawberry jam | Cook to ~223°F/106°C | Macerate fruit+sugar 6-12h first; final jam target 60-65% sugar overall |
| Kumquat candying | 185°F/85°C poach, staged over 7 days | Never jump straight to high sugar — shrivels fruit |

## Blanching / Poaching Times

| Item | Time | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Almonds/hazelnuts/pistachios (skin removal) | 2-3 min boil | Loosen skins |
| Peaches (peeling) | 20 sec boil | Loosen skin |
| Chestnuts (peeling) | 15 min soak + 5 min roast at 480°F/250°C | Loosen shell + pellicle |
| Citrus (pre-candying/marmalade) | 5 min boil, repeat 3-4x fresh water | Strip bitterness |
| Oysters (poaching) | To ~149°F/65°C, until edges curl | Avoid rubbery texture — very gentle |
| Monkfish (steaming) | 8-10 min | Remove immediately — carryover overcooks fast |

## Ripening & Storage

- **Climacteric fruits** (ripen after picking, emit ethylene): apricots, avocados, bananas, peaches, nectarines, apples, pears, plums, tomatoes. Speed-ripen by bagging with a banana/apple.
- **Non-climacteric fruits** (must be picked ripe, sensitive to ethylene, spoil faster near climacteric fruit): citrus, berries. **Never store these next to bananas/apples/pears.**
- Nuts: keep longer whole/in-shell; ground/sliced nuts go rancid faster — buy vacuum-sealed, store airtight and cold, refrigerate once opened.
- Cut fruit: refrigerate airtight, use within 24h; coat browning-prone fruit (apple, banana, peach, pear, apricot) in lemon/citrus juice first.

## Oxidation / Browning — Which Fruits & Fix

**Browns quickly when cut**: apples, bananas, peaches, pears, apricots.
**Fix**: coat with lemon juice, vinegar, or another citrus juice immediately after cutting; prep just before use rather than ahead of time.

## Zest vs. Peel — Decision Guide

- **Zest only** (colored outer layer, peeler or microplane): flavoring, garnish, infusing — avoid all white pith (bitter).
- **Full peel** (zest + pith + membrane removed together, chef's knife following the curve): needed before segmenting/supreming for salads or plating, or before candying whole citrus.
- Always use **untreated/organic** fruit when the zest itself will be eaten.

## Candying / Preserving Quick Rules

- Fruit candying (kumquats) works by **gradually** raising sugar concentration over multiple days — a sudden high-sugar syrup pulls water out too fast and shrivels the fruit.
- Jam: **macerate fruit + sugar first** (6-12h) to draw out juice before cooking — gives better texture and flavor than cooking straight away.
- Marmalade needs **fresh-water blanching repeated 3-4 times** before poaching, specifically to mellow the pith's bitterness.
- Gelatin must dissolve into liquid **no hotter than 158°F/70°C**, or it loses setting power.
- Butterfat matters: standard butter ≥82%; laminated doughs (puff pastry, croissant, brioche feuilletée) need **84%** butterfat for clean, distinct layers.
