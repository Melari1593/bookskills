# Chapter 7: Fruits with Seeds — Recipe Index

Source: FERRANDI Paris, *Fruits & Nuts*, Recipes > Fruits with Seeds (book pp. 182-201). 10 recipes: apples, pears, quinces, figs, melon, watermelon, grapes, and kiwifruit (pomes and other seed-containing fruits).

## Tarte Tatin
Serves 4. An elevated, deconstructed take on the classic upside-down apple tart.
Components: spiced speculaas-style shortbread crust (orange zest, cinnamon, nutmeg, clove, green anise, quatre-épices), apples first poached in a butter-vanilla syrup then caramelized in concentric circles in a cake pan, whipped vanilla cream, tempered white chocolate decoration, neutral glaze, gold leaf.
Notable: unlike a traditional Tatin, the caramelized-apple "topping" is baked separately and assembled onto a pre-baked spiced shortbread base rather than baked together.

## Tonka-Flavored Apple Crumble — *Crumble fleur de pomme tonka*
Serves 6.
Components: apple compote, almond-tonka-bean crumble, "apple caviar" (warm apple juice + agar-agar syrup dripped via syringe/pipette into ice-cold oil — thermal shock forms caviar-like pearls, then rinsed and strained), thin raw apple slices arranged in overlapping circles as a flower.
Draws on: *Making an Apple Compote* (ch. 4, p. 94).
Notable: teaches an oil-bath spherification method for fruit "caviar" (no sodium alginate needed) — reusable for other fruit juices.

## Pear Tiramisu — *Tiramisu aux poires*
Serves 6. A pear reinterpretation of tiramisu (no coffee-soaked ladyfingers).
Components: flourless chocolate-almond sponge, siphon-aerated vanilla mascarpone cream, chocolate crémeux, vanilla-poached pear brunoise, pear sorbet, tempered chocolate triangle decoration.

## Pears Belle Hélène — *Poire façon Belle Hélène*
Serves 8. Classic dessert, reworked into individual composed plates.
Components: poached pears (matured 12 hrs), vanilla ice cream frozen in a slab then cut into rings with round cutters, Belle Hélène chocolate sauce, a cooked diced-apple-vanilla-lime filling, almond tuiles draped over the top as a cap.
Notable: cutting ice cream rings from a frozen slab with cookie cutters is a clean way to get a neat composed shape without a ring mold.

## Quince Éclairs — *Éclairs aux coings*
Makes 6.
Components: quince jelly (made with 1 kg quinces), a quince "Tatin" topping (dry caramel poured into éclair-shaped mold cavities, then diced cooked quince set in), classic choux pastry piped into 5-in lengths, crème fraîche filling, baby watercress.
Draws on: *Making Quince Jelly* (ch. 4, p. 104).
Chef's notes/technique: bake choux without opening the oven door for the first 20 minutes — opening early collapses the rise.

## Figs in Red Wine Jelly with Stilton Ice Cream — *Figues sur gelée de vin rouge et crème glacée au Stilton*
Serves 6. An unusual sweet-savory pairing.
Components: Stilton blue cheese ice cream (custard base, matured 12 hrs), gelatin-set red wine jelly, fresh figs, walnuts, red-veined sorrel, and edible "autumn leaves" — rice paper sprayed with a red-to-yellow cocoa butter gradient using a velvet spray gun.
Notable: the rice-paper + colored-cocoa-butter spray technique is a reusable method for realistic edible leaf/petal decorations.

## Melon Gazpacho — *Gaspacho de melon*
Serves 6. Savory chilled soup.
Components: seeded melon blended with basil, olive oil, cayenne, and raspberry vinegar; served with Parmesan tuiles (baked in a leaf-imprinted silicone mold), crisp seared pata negra (Ibérico ham) chips, fresh basil.
Draws on: *Removing Melon Seeds* (ch. 2, p. 37).

## Watermelon Granita — *Granité de pastèque*
Serves 6. A pure technique showcase for the fork-scrape granita method.
Components: watermelon juice/puree + simple syrup, frozen in a shallow container and scraped with a fork every ~30 min over ~2 hours to build fine, separate ice crystals.
Key parameter: target **17° Brix** (checked with a refractometer) for the ideal icy-but-scoopable texture.

## Foie Gras with Grape Sauce Vierge and Cardamom Broth — *Foie gras sauté, vierge de raisin et bouillon cardamome noire*
Serves 4. Savory main course.
Components: grape "sauce vierge" (peeled Muscat grapes + golden raisins + olive oil + kaffir lime + lime juice, macerated 2+ hrs), a cardamom-ginger-kaffir lime-soy-mirin broth (infused 30 min, strained), pan-seared foie gras, snow peas, shiitake and enoki mushrooms, scallions.
Notable: one of the book's few savory grape applications — grapes used raw, macerated in a vinaigrette-style "sauce vierge" rather than cooked.

## Kiwifruit Vacherin — *Vacherin kiwi*
Serves 6. Classic French vacherin (layered meringue, ice cream, whipped cream), reworked with kiwifruit.
Components: kiwifruit-and-lemon-verbena sorbet (matured 12 hrs), mascarpone ice cream, vanilla mascarpone whipped cream, dried kiwifruit slices, a reduced-sugar meringue baked low (175°F/80°C) and stabilized with xanthan gum instead of the usual full sugar load, fresh green and gold kiwi wedges.
Notable: the xanthan-gum-stabilized reduced-sugar meringue is a reusable technique whenever a lighter/less-sweet meringue component is wanted.
