# Chapter 5: Citrus Fruits — Recipe Index

Source: FERRANDI Paris, *Fruits & Nuts*, Recipes > Citrus Fruits (book pp. 128-155). 13 recipes, restaurant/pâtisserie-level plated desserts (plus two savory seafood dishes). Reference index only — see the book for full ingredient quantities and step-by-step method.

## Hesperidium Lemon and Lime Tart — *Tarte au citron Hespéride*
Makes 2 tarts (serves 3 each). Active 4 hrs, freeze overnight.
Components: Swiss meringue, gelatin-set lemon mousse, lime cream/crémeux, genoise almond sponge, siphon-aerated sponge, white chocolate velvet spray, neutral glaze, marzipan collar, dried lemon/lime slices, finger lime "caviar."
Draws on: citrus segmenting/zesting (ch. 3), chocolate tempering, sponge cooked via whipped-cream siphon.
Notable: named for the *hesperidium* (botanical term for the citrus fruit type); a modern deconstructed tart assembled from ~8 separate sub-components rather than a single filled shell.

## Spiced Babas with Orange Compote — *Baba, compotée d'oranges, infusion aux épices douces*
Serves 6. Active 2 hrs; many parallel overnight/multi-hour chills.
Components: yeasted baba dough soaked in a cinnamon/star anise/orange-blossom syrup, orange compote (segmented oranges stewed 2 hrs on very low heat), orange sorbet (matured 12 hrs, then churned), mascarpone cream marbles, tempered white chocolate discs, caramelized grissini.
Draws on: citrus segmenting (ch. 3, p. 68), sorbet method and sugar-maturation principle (ch. 2).
Notable: soaking syrup is cooled to 140°F/60°C before the babas go in — too hot and it overcooks the crumb.

## Mandarins, Egg White Foam, and Olive Oil and Vanilla Ice Cream — *Mandarine, blancs mousseux et crème glacée à l'huile d'olive et vanille*
Serves 6. Active 2 hrs, cooking ~5 hrs total.
Components: mandarin marmalade, mandarin shortbread cookies, madeleine sponge, meringues, dried mandarin zest, lemon-mandarin jelly (agar-agar set), vanilla-and-olive-oil ice cream, citrus vinaigrette, candied mandarins, gelatin-stabilized Italian-meringue-style egg white foam.
Draws on: citrus marmalade method (ch. 4), zest drying (ch. 4).
Notable: uses olive oil as a primary ice cream flavor alongside vanilla — a savory-leaning pairing.

## Kumquat Cake — *Cake au kumquat*
Serves 6. Active 45 min, cook 35 min. Storage: up to 3 days.
Components: candied kumquats (tossed in flour before folding in), simple almond-flour pound cake batter, kumquat-zest soaking syrup brushed on after baking, calamansi jelly (agar-agar) set in a frame directly on top of the cake, dried candied kumquat garnish.
Draws on: *Candying Kumquats* (ch. 4, p. 84), *Drying Citrus Slices* (ch. 4, p. 122).
Notable: the simplest, most home-cook-friendly recipe in this chapter — a good starting point for practicing the candying technique.

## Sugar-Glazed Yuzu Madeleines — *Madeleine au yuzu, glaçage au sucre*
Makes 24.
Components: madeleine batter (rested 2-12 hrs, ideally overnight), yuzu filling (pectin + agar-agar set), sugar glaze (confectioners' sugar + yuzu juice).
Key parameters: two-stage bake — 375°F/190°C for 2 min, then drop to 340°F/170°C for 7 min — the classic technique for forcing the madeleine's characteristic domed "bump." Batter chilled 10 min in the pan just before baking for the same reason.
Notable: batter rest time (longer = better rise/bump) is the one variable worth not skipping.

## Kaffir Lime Praline and Marshmallow Chocolate Bars — *Tablette chocolat combawa, guimauve et praliné*
Makes 2 bars.
Components: kaffir-lime hazelnut praline paste, kaffir-lime marshmallow (gelatin + sugar cooked to 230°F/110°C, whipped), tempered dark chocolate shell (melt to 122°F/50°C, cool to 82-84°F/28-29°C, rework to 88-90°F/31-32°C).
Draws on: praline-paste method (ch. 4) — note the added constraint here that processed praline must stay under 95°F/35°C or the hazelnut oil separates.
Chef's notes: the egg-white-free marshmallow can be reheated and reused if it sets up before use.

## Langoustine with Finger Lime Caviar and Chia Tuiles — *Langoustine, citron caviar et tuile de chia*
Serves 4. Savory.
Components: dill oil, strained yogurt broth (curds/whey split by heating past curdling), fromage frais cream, chia tuiles (chia soaked 24 hrs, dried, then fried crisp), pickled cucumber, apple brunoise, briefly seared langoustines (2-3 min, belly-side down), finger lime pulp as a citrus "caviar" garnish.
Notable: one of only two savory dishes in the Citrus chapter; finger lime is used purely as a garnish for its caviar-like citrus pearls, not as a cooking ingredient.

## Grapefruit Yule Log — *Bûche au pamplemousse*
Serves 6.
Components: pear mousse, rolled joconde almond sponge, grapefruit crémeux (custard set with gelatin and white chocolate), grapefruit syrup, candied grapefruit zest (cut with the channel/canelle knife, then triple-blanched to strip bitterness), fresh grapefruit segments, canned pear in syrup.
Draws on: *Canelling* (ch. 3, p. 64), *Blanching Citrus Fruits* (ch. 4) — triple-blanching zest in fresh water each time is the standard bitterness fix reused across multiple citrus recipes in this book.

## King Crab with Pomelos, Avocado, and Grapefruit Clouds
Savory.
Components: spicy mayonnaise (hand-emulsified egg yolk + Thai chili paste), avocado puree, grapefruit "veils" (thin gelatin/agar-agar sheets set on acetate), dehydrated cucumber powder, king crab merus, pomelo segments, a whipped-cream-siphon "cloud" garnish.
Notable: the second savory citrus dish; uses a whipped-cream siphon for a savory foam rather than a dessert application.

## Buddha's-Hand Mille-Feuille — *Millefeuille à la main de bouddha*
Serves 6. Active 4½ hrs, chill 8 hrs.
Components: set/frozen gianduja cubes, fully laminated puff pastry (classic *détrempe* + butter block, given roughly 2½-3 "turns" with chilling between each), ground caramel powder, candied Buddha's-hand citron, lemon diplomat cream (pastry cream + gelatin + whipped cream), gianduja crémeux.
Notable: this is the book's fullest worked example of classic puff-pastry lamination (fold-in-three, rotate 90°, chill, repeat) — worth cross-referencing for any other laminated-dough recipe.

## Bergamot Puff Pastry Brioche Knots
Makes 10. Spans two days (overnight chill + same-day 4 hr chill/rise).
Components: bergamot marmalade (made a day ahead), a laminated brioche dough (butter block enclosed and given puff-pastry-style turns), braided into knots filled with marmalade, proofed 2½ hrs at 82°F/28°C, baked 340°F/170°C 15-20 min, brushed with syrup, finished with diced candied bergamot.
Draws on: *Making Citrus Marmalade* (ch. 4, p. 101). Notable: a hybrid technique — laminating an enriched yeasted dough the same way as puff pastry.

## Porto-Vecchio Ice Cream Cones — *Face u caldu, cornet de Porto-Vecchio*
Serves 6. A Corsican-themed recipe.
Components: hazelnut praline ice cream (staged sugar/glucose additions, cooked to 185°F/85°C, matured 12 hrs), citron sorbet (same staged method, matured 4+ hrs, citron juice folded in after), candied citron zest (poached briefly then oven-dried ~2 hrs at 105°F/40°C into curved shapes), handmade waffle cones (soy-lecithin batter, shaped immediately around a cone roller).
Chef's notes: top with crushed canistrelli cookies for the full Corsican effect.

## Yogurt and Granola Parfaits with Calamansi Pearls — *Yogourt, perles de kalamansi et granola*
Serves 4.
Components: homemade fermented yogurt (8-10 hrs in a yogurt maker, or an oven-off residual-heat method if you don't have one), homemade granola (oats, puffed rice, pistachios, Piedmont hazelnuts, sesame, dried currants/cranberries, baked 300°F/150°C 25-30 min), calamansi pearls.
Notable: the yogurt-maker-free fermentation trick (ferment in a 105°F/40°C oven 2 hrs, then leave in the switched-off oven undisturbed 6 more hours) is reusable for any home-fermentation task.
