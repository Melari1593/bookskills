# Chapter 8: Berries and Rhubarb — Recipe Index

Source: FERRANDI Paris, *Fruits & Nuts*, Recipes > Berries and Rhubarb (book pp. 204-227). 12 recipes covering strawberries, raspberries, blackberries, black currants, blueberries, red currants, lingonberries, acai, cranberries, and rhubarb.

## Strawberries with Strawberry and Basil Sorbet — *Fraises fraîches, jus glacé et sorbet fraise basilic*
Serves 6. A multi-varietal strawberry tasting (Gariguette, Fraises des Bois, Mara des Bois, pineberries).
Components: dried/ground basil powder, optional reduced-sugar meringue petals, clear chilled strawberry juice (strawberries drained through a sieve without pressing, for clarity), strawberry-basil sorbet, a thin strawberry "paper" (gelatin sheet).
Notable: built around showcasing several strawberry varieties side by side rather than a single composed technique.

## Strawberry Layer Cake — *Fraisier*
Serves 6. The classic French fraisier.
Components: almond genoise sponge, mousseline cream (pastry cream + butter), reduced strawberry juice, pistachio praline paste, a strawberry-juice-tinted marzipan collar, whole strawberries lining the ring.
Key technique note: the pastry cream and the butter must be at the **same temperature (~64-68°F/18-20°C)** when combined, or the mousseline cream will split or turn grainy.

## Raspberry Opéra Cakes — *Opéra framboise*
Serves 6. A raspberry reinterpretation of the classic Opéra.
Components: chocolate-raspberry ganache, raspberry crémeux, raspberry confit, joconde sponge, raspberry soaking syrup, whipped vanilla ganache, tempered chocolate decoration, edible gold luster dust, neutral glaze.

## Blackberry Charlotte — *Charlotte aux mûres et biscuits de Reims*
Serves 6. A classic charlotte lined with pink champagne biscuits (*biscuits roses de Reims*) instead of standard ladyfingers.
Components: gelatin-set blackberry mousse, blackberry soaking syrup, house-made ladyfinger sponge, a gelatin-set blackberry insert, dried rose petals and edible sorrel/oxalis garnish.

## Black Currant Pavlovas — *Pavlova yaourt cassis*
Makes 6.
Components: black currant frozen yogurt (churned custard base, hand-marbled with black currant puree), Swiss meringue piped into "nest" shapes and slow-baked 3-4 hrs at 175°F/80°C, a pectin-set black currant compote, fromage blanc cream (whipped cream + fromage blanc + lemon zest + vanilla).

## Blueberry Cheesecake — *Cheesecake aux myrtilles*
Serves 8. A baked New-York-style cheesecake.
Components: a from-scratch shortcrust base (baked, ground to crumbs, bound with clarified butter rather than store-bought cookies), cream cheese filling baked 330°F/170°C for 1 hr **without opening the oven door**, with a bowl of water on the lower rack for humidity (prevents cracking), a pectin-set blueberry puree, edible gold leaf.
Chef's notes: the cheesecake is better the next day; cool it slowly or it can sink in the center; a clarified-butter method is given (melt, skim froth, pour off the clear yellow layer).

## Panna Cotta with Red Currants and Cilantro — *Groseilles et coriandre*
Serves 6. An unusual herb-forward composition using cilantro as a sweet-dessert flavor.
Components: cilantro powder (blanched then dried), cilantro "opalines" (thin cooked-sugar/fondant discs), hazelnut shortbread, a pectin-set red currant gel, vanilla panna cotta, red currant sorbet, cilantro-flavored frozen ice balls, fresh red currants.
Notable: demonstrates using an herb (cilantro) as a genuine dessert flavor across multiple textures (powder, opaline, sorbet-adjacent ball), not just garnish.

## Red Currant and Mixed Berry Tart
Serves 6.
Components: shortcrust pastry, a frangipane-style almond cream filling baked with red currants pressed into it, gelatin-set mascarpone cream, and a decorative mixed-berry topping (red currants, green gooseberries, raspberries, white raspberries, blackberries, blueberries).

## Nordic Lingonberry Parfaits — *Dessert Nordique aux airelles*
Serves 6. Scandinavian-inspired layered glass dessert.
Components: lingonberries marinated in chestnut syrup and lime juice, oat-almond-cranberry granola, chestnut diplomat cream, vanilla cream, a gelatin-set lingonberry jelly, a piped vanilla-chestnut paste line.

## Acai and Black Currant Energy Bowls
A "smoothie bowl" format built on a proper Italian-meringue-based mousse rather than a simple blended puree.
Components: acai and black currant mousse (hot sugar syrup poured into whipping egg whites to make a meringue, then folded with fruit puree and whipped cream), fresh mango cut into ribbons with a peeler, sliced kiwifruit and banana, pomegranate seeds, edible flower garnish.
Draws on: *Cutting Mangos* (ch. 3, p. 56), *Cutting and Seeding Pomegranates* (ch. 3, p. 62).

## Cranberry and Beet Juice — *Jus de cranberry et betterave*
Makes 4 cups (1 L). The simplest recipe in this chapter — a cold-pressed juice/mocktail.
Components: candied rose petals (egg white + sugar coated, air-set) for garnish, fresh beets + cranberries + raspberries run through a juicer, grated ginger, rose water, cane sugar syrup, chilled and served over ice.

## Rhubarb, Berry, and Pink Praline Tart — *Tarte boulangère à la rhubarbe, fruits rouges et pralines roses*
Serves 6. A "bakery-style" tart built on an enriched pink-praline brioche base (true kneaded bread dough, ~20 min total kneading) instead of pastry.
Components: pink praline brioche dough, poached rhubarb, a rhubarb-raspberry-hibiscus jam, diplomat cream, a classic French "Saint-Tropez" crumb topping (as used on brioche des rois/tropézienne), fresh strawberries, raspberries, red currants, elderflowers, pink pralines.
Notable: pink pralines (*pralines roses*) are a Lyonnais candy-almond specialty used here as both a dough mix-in and garnish — distinctive regional ingredient.
