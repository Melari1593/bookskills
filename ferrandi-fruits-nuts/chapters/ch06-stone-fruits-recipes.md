# Chapter 6: Stone Fruits — Recipe Index

Source: FERRANDI Paris, *Fruits & Nuts*, Recipes > Stone Fruits (book pp. 158-179). 11 recipes covering apricots, peaches, nectarines, plums (mirabelle/greengage/damson/yellow), cherries, and olives (botanically also a drupe/stone fruit).

## Apricot, Pistachio, and Green Almond Tart — *Tarte abricot, pistaches et amandes fraîches*
Serves 6.
Components: candied rosemary (egg-white + sugar coated, dried 12 hrs at 120°F/50°C), almond shortcrust (made with the *fraisage* smearing technique), almond-pistachio frangipane-style cream, fresh apricot quarters baked directly on the cream (325°F/160°C, 25 min), fresh blanched/peeled/sliced almonds and pistachio flour as garnish.
Draws on: *Blanching Almonds* (ch. 2).

## Peach Flower Tart — *Fleur de pêche*
Serves 8.
Components: sweet shortcrust brushed with melted white chocolate (moisture barrier so the crust stays crisp), peaches poached in red wine (preferably Syrah) with vanilla, lemon-verbena-infused whipped cream, thin raw peach slices rolled into decorative "roses" (dipped in syrup to prevent browning first).
Notable: the white-chocolate-brushed-crust trick is reusable for any wet-filling tart.

## Nectarine and Lemon Verbena Religieuses — *Religieuse nectarine, verveine*
Serves 6. A reinterpretation of the classic stacked choux religieuse.
Components: choux buns with craquelin topping, vanilla ganache, lemon-verbena pastry cream, nectarine compote, frozen glazed nectarine "balls," white chocolate disks, chocolate ganache piped as alternating-fruit-color "petals."
Notable: an advanced multi-texture composition — good reference for combining choux, ganache, and fruit compote in one plated piece.

## Nectarine Melba — *Brugnon Melba*
Serves 10. A nectarine riff on the classic Peach Melba.
Components: fresh almond jelly (almonds ground with milk, matured 24 hrs, strained to almond milk, then set), nectarine ice cream (matured 12 hrs), oven-dried raspberries, nectarines poached at 176°F/80°C for 20 min, orange-blossom mascarpone cream.
Chef's notes: pipe the mascarpone cream into a neat ring by setting the plate on a rotating cake turner while piping.

## Mirabelle Plum Panna Cotta
Components: mirabelle jam (fruit + honey, rosemary infused via a fabric tea bag), a chocolate-praline-puffed-rice crumb topping, tuiles baked on a leaf-impression silicone mat for a decorative curved shape, and a two-tone panna cotta poured in two stages at opposing angles (glasses tilted in a "nest" of crumpled paper towel or egg cartons) to create a clean V-shaped layer line.
Notable: the two-angle-pour technique for a layered look in a glass is reusable for any layered chilled dessert.

## Greengage Plum Turnovers — *Chausson aux prunes Reine-Claude*
Serves 6. Classic French puff-pastry turnover (chausson).
Components: full laminated puff pastry (détrempe + butter block, single turns with chilling between each), greengage compote made via dry caramel (sugar cooked to caramel with no added water, then butter and fruit stirred in), caramel powder garnish (ground set caramel).
Draws on: puff pastry lamination, same family of technique as the Buddha's-Hand Mille-Feuille (ch. 5) and Bergamot Brioche Knots (ch. 5).

## Yellow Plum Clafoutis — *Clafoutis aux prunes jaunes*
Serves 5. The simplest recipe in this chapter — good entry point.
Components: halved plums baked directly into a custardy batter (eggs, sugar, almond flour, AP flour, milk, crème fraîche, melted butter), baked 400°F/210°C for 30 min, dusted with confectioners' sugar. Chef's notes suggest serving with vanilla whipped cream on the side.

## Basque Cake with Damson Plum Cream — *Gâteau basque aux quetsches*
Serves 6.
Components: sweet shortcrust pastry, a damson-plum pastry cream filling (cooked like crème pâtissière with plum puree stirred in), a decorative cross pattern cut into the top crust using a reusable stencil (cut from an inexpensive silicone baking mat), the stencil cavity filled with damson jam after baking.
Notable: the "cut your own reusable stencil from a cheap silicone mat" trick applies to decorating any laminated or shortcrust top crust.

## Cherries Jubilee — *Jubilé de cerises*
Serves 6. Classic French flambé dessert.
Components: vanilla ice cream (staged sugar/glucose additions, matured 12 hrs), crisp tuiles, flambéed cherries (cherry puree reduced to a thick syrup, butter and kirsch added, then flambéed tableside with a long-reach lighter).

## Black Forest Gâteau — *Forêt-noire*
Serves 8. Assembled in a Yule-log (bûche) mold rather than a round cake ring.
Components: chocolate sponge, chocolate mousse (crème anglaise base cooked to 181-185°F/83-85°C, folded with whipped cream), mascarpone cream, tempered dark chocolate sheets, pectin-set cherry jelly, kirsch. Freeze overnight; thaw ~3 hrs before serving.
Draws on: chocolate tempering curve referenced from the Kaffir Lime Bars technique (ch. 5).

## Taggiasca Olive Tapenade Swirls
A savory laminated viennoiserie (croissant-dough swirl) — included here because olives are botanically a drupe (stone fruit).
Components: laminated yeasted dough (given puff-pastry-style turns, chilled ~6 hrs total), Taggiasca olive and caper tapenade, beurre noisette (brown butter) brushed on before a high-heat bake (~450°F/Gas Mark 8, 20 min).
Chef's notes: stir in toasted ground almonds once cooled, or add fresh basil in summer, to vary the tapenade.
